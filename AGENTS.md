Agent Guide – dresslikemommy

Scope and purpose
- This file applies to the entire repository. It explains how agents should work on this theme and how to resume work across sessions.

Principles
- Evidence-first: Base every finding on repo files; never invent facts.
- Minimal, focused changes: Keep edits surgical and consistent with Dawn style.
- Shopify constraints: No secrets in theme; no server code in Liquid; use App Proxies for backend.
- Observability: Always leave notes in ops/AGENT_WORKLOG.md when you change or defer something. Follow `ops/MEMORY_CONTINUITY_PROTOCOL.md` so future agents know what was already fixed, what remains blocked, and what must not be redone blindly. Use `ops/PROBLEM_TRACKER.md` for any active problem, repeated blocker, failed readback, or unresolved diagnostic.
- Problem-found means fix-now: When an agent finds a real issue, stale platform diagnostic, broken workflow, failed readback, or blocked lane, the default is to fix it immediately inside the approved scope, or route around it while continuing other useful work. Do not treat `blocked`, `monitor`, `needs refresh`, or `platform/credential issue` as permission to stop and do nothing. Open or update a `ops/PROBLEM_TRACKER.md` entry with status, attempts, evidence, failed paths, next action, gates, and fixed criteria. A lane may be parked only after at least two grounded recovery paths are tried or ruled out, the exact blocker is documented, the next concrete unblock action is named, and independent lanes keep moving.
