## 2. Information We Collect

When you shop with us, create an account, or get in touch, you may provide:

- Contact information — name, email address, phone number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Payment information — credit/debit card details (processed securely by Shopify Payments; we never see or store your full card number)

### Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically:

- Device and browser information — browser type, operating system, screen resolution, device type
- IP address and approximate location (city/region level)
- Browsing activity — pages visited, products viewed, time spent on pages, referring website
- Online identifiers and advertising identifiers (e.g., cookie IDs, mobile ad IDs)
- Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5)

### Information from Third Parties

We may receive limited information from:

- **Shopify** — our e-commerce platform
- **Payment processors** — transaction confirmation and fraud prevention
- **Advertising and marketing platforms** — if you interact with our ads (e.g., click-through data, conversion data)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- Process and fulfill your orders
- Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and receipts
- Respond to your questions and support requests
- Improve our website, products, and shopping experience
- Send marketing emails (only with your consent where required)
- **Measure advertising performance, attribute conversions, and deliver personalized advertising** on platforms such as Google (Google Ads, YouTube, Search, Display, Gmail) and other advertising partners
- **Build audiences of existing or similar customers** so we can reach you and people like you with relevant offers
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations
- Personalize your shopping experience

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, under some U.S. state privacy laws (including California's CCPA/CPRA), the use of advertising cookies and the sharing of hashed identifiers with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered a "sale" or "share." You can opt out — see Section 8.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising Partners

We share limited personal information with the following categories of service providers and partners, only as needed to operate our business:

- **Shopify** — powers our online store and processes orders
- **Shopify Payments / Stripe** — PCI-DSS compliant payment processing
- **Email marketing providers** — newsletter delivery; unsubscribe anytime
- **Shipping carriers** — deliver your order and provide tracking
- **Google Analytics** — aggregated site usage data
- **Google Ads, including Google Customer Match and conversion-based audience features** — we share hashed customer data such as email addresses, phone numbers, and address fields, plus conversion and event data, with Google so that we can:
  - measure the performance of our ads and attribute purchases,
  - build audiences of existing customers (e.g., past purchasers, cart abandoners),
  - reach similar audiences across Google services (Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Maps, Discover), and
  - exclude existing customers from certain campaigns.

  Google acts as our service provider/processor for this purpose and is contractually restricted to using the data on our behalf. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before sharing this data. You can opt out of personalized Google ads at any time at https://adssettings.google.com and manage cookie/consent choices via the cookie banner on our site.

- **Other advertising and analytics partners** — we may use additional advertising, retargeting, and analytics services. Where these involve cross-context behavioral advertising, they are subject to your consent / opt-out choices described in Sections 5 and 8.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not allow our service providers or advertising partners to use your information for their own independent marketing purposes.

## 5. Cookies, Tracking, and Consent

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- keep your cart active and remember preferences (strictly necessary),
- understand site traffic and performance (analytics),
- support marketing, advertising, retargeting, and conversion measurement (advertising — only with consent where required).

**Consent banner:** If you visit our site from a region that requires consent (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or certain U.S. states), you will see a cookie/consent banner that lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. We honor those choices, including via Google Consent Mode, before any advertising or analytics tags fire.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings and opt out of personalized ads at https://adssettings.google.com or http://optout.aboutads.info.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Dress Like Mommy sells children's clothing, but our website is not directed at children. All purchases must be made by an adult (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16 (or under 13 in the United States). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

All customers can access, correct, unsubscribe from marketing, and request deletion of their data.

**GDPR (EEA / UK / Switzerland):** You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**CCPA / CPRA (California) and other U.S. state privacy laws:** You have the right to know what we collect, delete your data, correct inaccurate data, limit the use of sensitive personal information, and **opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising**. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out request.

**To exercise any right, including the right to opt out of sale/share:**

- Use the **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link in the footer of our site, or
- Click "Reject" on the cookie/consent banner, or
- Email us at info@dresslikemommy.com.

We will respond within 30 days (or 45 days for CCPA requests, as permitted by law). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
