# GSC indexing diagnosis — dresslikemommy.com (read-only)

Date: 2026-09-29. Source: Search Console, property `sc-domain:dresslikemommy.com`, Page indexing report (data last updated 2026-09-20), Performance (last 3 months), Sitemaps; live checks of robots.txt, sitemap, and sample URLs; theme code in `snippets/meta-tags.liquid` and `layout/theme.liquid`. Nothing was changed in Search Console, Shopify or git.

Limits: GSC shows at most 1,000 example URLs per reason, so URL-pattern shares below are sample shares (n=1,000, or the full list when the reason has fewer). "Trend" = the report's chart: the Not-indexed total is flat at about 22-23K and Indexed flat at about 7K from 6/30 to 9/20; per-reason mini-trends could not be read as text. Every reason shows Validation "Not Started" except Discovered ("Passed"). 404 was first detected 2022-08-16.

## Headline

- 7,076 indexed vs 22,983 not indexed. The 22,983 is NOT a 22,983-page problem. Only about 9K of it is URLs we want indexed and Google has not taken (mostly Crawled-not-indexed localized pages). The rest is dead or old URLs, parameter/variant URLs, redirects, feeds and Shopify system URLs.
- Live site has about 442 sitemap URLs per language (301 products, 50 collections, 74 blogs, 17 pages) across 21 language versions (English plus 20). GSC says the sitemap has 7,516 discovered pages, and 7,076 pages are indexed in total. Most of the sitemap is therefore already indexed.
- Traffic is localized. Last 3 months: 2.85K clicks, 139K impressions. US = 350 clicks / 67K impressions. Greece, Denmark, Norway, Netherlands, Israel, Italy, Czechia, Romania, Poland together = about 1,900 clicks. Top pages by clicks are localized collections and blogs (`/el/blogs/news/mommy-and-me-matching-outfit-ideas` 176 clicks, `/no/collections/dresses` 114, `/da/collections/dresses` 105, `/nl/collections/dresses` 100). About 92% of a 1,000-URL sample of indexed pages is localized. So the localized pages are the traffic, and the localized pages Google refuses to index (Crawled - currently not indexed) are the only part of this report that can move traffic.

## What is harmless (do not spend time on)

Alternate page with proper canonical (5,632), Page with redirect (979), Blocked by robots.txt (660), Excluded by noindex for `/services/` (264) and utility pages (about 100), `.atom` duplicates (131), Blocked other 4xx (12), and most 404s that are parameter variants of dead handles. Together about 8,000-8,500 of the 22,983. Fixing these will change the report number but not traffic.

## Ranked fixes

Tags: ENGINE = `organic_engine.py` redirect/SEO meta lanes; MAIN = theme code or a main CEO session; OWNER = needs the owner.

1. MAIN + OWNER: attack Crawled-not-indexed on the localized pages that are worth indexing (6,046 pages, 96% localized). This is a quality and demand signal from Google, not a technical block (self-canonical, hreflang present, in sitemap, 200). Levers: (a) prioritize languages that already rank (no, da, nl, el, he, it, cs, ro, pl, fi, ko) and internally link their new localized collections and blogs from already-indexed localized pages and the language homepages; (b) tighten translated collection intros and product titles for pt, es, it, hi, fr, de, ru (the biggest not-indexed languages) so they are not thin or near-duplicate of English, which is a Translate & Adapt task (OWNER approves, MAIN executes); (c) the CEO loop keeps requesting indexing for the top 10 pages per run (already running). Do not noindex anything here without evidence, because the same pattern is what indexes and earns the clicks in other languages. Estimated recoverable: 1,000-1,500 pages (about 15-25% of the bucket), about +15K-25K impressions per quarter (+11-18%) and about +300-500 clicks per quarter, extrapolated from average indexed-page yield (about 20 impressions per page per quarter) weighted toward collections and blogs. Confidence: low-medium. Cannot be forced.
2. ENGINE: redirect the 66 dead product handles that still 404 to the closest live collection (list in `gsc_404_product_handle_status.tsv`, status 404 rows). A root-path redirect also covers all 20 language prefixes automatically (verified on 276 sampled URLs). Largest first: `iris-bloom-family-matching-set`, `lavender-plaid-family-matching-set-tank-dress-shirt-2`, `fruit-green-family-matching-set`, `garden-floral-family-matching-set`, the Christmas pajama/jumper handles (map to `christmas-pajamas` or `family-sweaters`), `mommy-me-matching-magical-unicorn-dress`, `mommy-and-me-off-shoulder-halter-top`, `mommy-me-matching-dots-dress`. The current cap is 10 per run, so about 7 hourly runs. Clears roughly 2,600-3,000 of 8,699 404s. Impact under 2K impressions per quarter; do it because it is cheap and cleans crawl waste. Add the 14 handles that are live in English but 404 only under retired languages (`father-and-son-matching-swim-trunks...`, `men-s-boys-...`, and so on) only if those retired-language paths are worth redirecting (see 3).
3. ENGINE: redirect retired-language paths (`/vi`, `/th`, `/id`, `/tr`, `/zh-hans`, `/zh-hant`, `/en-au`, `/en-ae`, `/en-at`) to the English equivalent (about 90 unique paths in the sample, for example `/vi/blogs/news/*`, `/zh-hans/blogs/news/*`). Low impact; only if these languages previously earned impressions. If not, leave as 404 (correct behavior).
4. OWNER/catalog (already C1): `christmas-tops` has 2 live products, so the theme noindexes it and Google rejected indexing. Fill it to at least 3 products or point Christmas top intent at `family-tops` and `christmas-sweaters`. Season clock: Christmas pages need to be live by mid-October. Highest revenue-relevant item in this report even though it is small in page count.
5. MAIN (verify first): `new-women-outfits` shows canonical to `matching-outfits` in English but self-canonical in `/es/`; and 155 URLs were reported noindexed. Confirm which is intended (`layout/theme.liquid` only maps `popular-family-matching` to `new-women-outfits`) and make the localized canonicals consistent with English so hreflang clusters are not contradictory. Also check the localized `mother-daughter-matching-dresses` near-duplicate (54 Google-chose-different-canonical).
6. MAIN: out-of-range `?page=N` should not return 200 with a self-canonical (29 soft 404s, and it invites more). In `snippets/meta-tags.liquid`, when `paginate.current_page > paginate.pages`, output noindex. Tiny impact.
7. MAIN: broken link sources producing malformed URLs: `/frpages/shipping-info`, `/itpages/...`, `/japages/...`, `/nlpages/...`, `/ptpages/...` (a JS patch in `layout/theme.liquid` around line 1076 repairs them client-side, but crawlers still follow the raw href; fix `snippets/shipping-country-confirmation.liquid` and any similar snippet to build `localized_root_url` with a trailing slash), and `/xx/pages/mail to:support@...` (an `href` missing `mailto:`). About 15 URLs; hygiene.
8. Skip: `.atom` duplicates, robots-blocked `/services/`, the alternate-canonical parameter URLs, page-with-redirect. If the owner wants a cleaner report, `Disallow: /*.atom` via `robots.txt.liquid` (MAIN, optional).

## Estimated recoverable, in one line

Realistic upside is from Fix 1 only: about +1,000-1,500 indexed localized pages, +15K-25K impressions per quarter, +300-500 clicks per quarter. Everything else is hygiene worth under about 2K impressions per quarter. Nothing here will turn the 22,983 into 7,000 indexed.
