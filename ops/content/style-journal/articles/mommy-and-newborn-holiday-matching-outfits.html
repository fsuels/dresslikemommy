The easiest way to match with a newborn at the holidays is to coordinate color and print instead of trying to find the exact same garment in two sizes. A new mom and a baby can share a palette (cream and red, forest green and plaid, soft neutrals), a pattern family (stripes with stripes, plaid with plaid) or one repeated detail, and still look like a pair in photos. This guide covers how to choose that shared look, what to check before ordering anything for a very small baby, and how to fold the rest of the family in.

Match the Palette, Not the Size Label

Many matching designs start at toddler or child sizes, and baby sizes are not offered on every design. Rather than hunting for an identical set, build the pair around one shared element:

  • One color family. Mom in cream or oatmeal, baby in cream with a touch of red. The eye reads them as a set.
  • One print. Buffalo plaid on one of you and a solid red or green on the other keeps it coordinated without being identical.
  • One repeated detail. A bow, a trim color or a Christmas motif on both outfits is enough.

Browse the mommy and me collection to see how designs are paired, then open individual product pages to see which sizes each one offers. Each product page lists its own size range, so check the smallest size shown before you fall in love with a design.

Outfit Ideas for Mom

Postpartum bodies change from week to week, so comfort and room matter more than a precise fit. A few directions that tend to work:

  • Soft pajamas. A pajama set is the most forgiving choice for a first holiday at home. The Christmas pajamas and family pajamas collections have sets in women's sizes that you can pair with a coordinating baby look.
  • A cozy sweater. A relaxed family sweater in a size up from your usual gives room to move and to hold a baby comfortably.
  • A sweatshirt for daytime. A matching-style family sweatshirt works for a visit with relatives or a short walk.

Use the size chart on each product page and compare it with clothes that fit you comfortably right now, not with your pre-pregnancy sizes.

Dressing a Newborn for the Photo

A newborn mostly sleeps, so the outfit has to be easy to put on and easy to take off again. These habits help:

  • Choose soft, stretchy fabric. Avoid scratchy trims near the neck.
  • Keep layers simple. One outfit plus a blanket in your palette is easier than several pieces.
  • Check closures. Snaps or zippers make diaper changes quick, which matters more than the print.
  • Wash before wearing. Follow the care label on each garment, especially for anything that will touch a baby's skin.
  • Follow your pediatrician's guidance on sleep. For sleeping, use whatever your baby's doctor recommends. The matching look is for awake time and photos.

Photo Ideas for Mom and Baby

  • By the tree. Sit on the floor with the baby in your lap and tree lights behind you.
  • In bed. A relaxed morning photo in matching-palette pajamas feels natural and needs no posing.
  • In a window. Soft daylight is flattering and faster than setting up lights.
  • Close detail shots. Hands together on a plaid sleeve tell the story even if faces are asleep.

Take a handful of photos quickly and stop. Babies fuss after a few minutes, and the best frame is often the one you did not plan. For more on sizing and styling a whole group, see our Baby's first Christmas matching family outfits guide.

Bringing the Rest of the Family In

Once mom and baby have a look, the rest of the family can borrow the same palette. A dad or older sibling in a coordinating sweater makes the photo feel like one group. A Simple Shopping Checklist

  • Pick the shared palette first, then choose the pieces.
  • Check the size range on each product page before you commit to a design.
  • Measure yourself now and use the size chart instead of guessing.
  • Choose one comfortable option for mom and one easy option for baby.
  • Decide who else will be in the photo, and pick their pieces from the same palette.

Timing: Order Early

A baby's size can change quickly, and holiday dates do not move. Each product page shows its estimated delivery window, so check it before you order and leave time to try everything on and fix a mismatch well before the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a newborn match with mom?

Yes, by matching color and print. Not every design is made in baby sizes, so check the size range on each product page and coordinate the palette when an identical set is not available.

What should a new mom wear for holiday photos?

Something soft and roomy that you can sit and hold a baby in comfortably, such as pajamas, a relaxed sweater or a sweatshirt, sized by the chart on the product page.

How many outfits does a newborn need for the holidays?

One photo outfit and a spare is plenty. Newborns spill and change often, so keep a second option in the same palette within reach.

How do I include the rest of the family?

Pick one shared color or print, then choose a coordinating piece for each person. They do not need to match exactly.
