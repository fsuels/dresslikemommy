A Christmas Eve box is a small box or basket the family opens the night before Christmas, and the easiest way to fill one is with the "wear, read, eat, do" formula: matching pajamas to wear, a book to read, a treat to eat and one simple activity to do together. Matching pajamas are the centerpiece because everyone can put them on straight away, which turns the opening into the first photo of the holiday. This guide covers what to include, how to choose and size the pajamas, how to reveal the box, and how to keep it simple enough that you will actually do it.

The Wear, Read, Eat, Do Formula

A box that tries to hold everything quickly becomes expensive and cluttered. Four items keep it focused:

  • Wear. One pair of pajamas per person, ideally in one shared print or a coordinated palette.
  • Read. A Christmas picture book for younger kids, or a short holiday story or poem everyone takes turns reading aloud.
  • Eat. Something small and shareable, like hot cocoa mix with a marshmallow, a few cookies to decorate, or a bag of popcorn for the movie.
  • Do. A low-effort activity: a holiday movie, a game, an ornament to hang, or a letter to leave out for Santa.

You can swap any slot to suit your family. The formula just stops the box from turning into a second round of gifts, so Christmas morning stays the main event.

Choosing the Pajamas for the Box

Because the pajamas are the biggest item, choose them first and build the rest around them. A few approaches work well:

  • One print for everyone. The same design in child and adult sizes gives the strongest group photo. Our Christmas pajamas collection has sets made for the whole family, and the family pajamas collection is wider if you want more prints to compare.
  • Coordinated, not identical. If tastes differ, pick two prints in the same red, green or cream palette so each person gets a pair they like.
  • Plaid and simple graphics. A buffalo plaid tree or a plain Christmas graphic photographs well in low light by the tree, and it is easy for a child to recognize the moment they pull it out of the box.

Sizing Pajamas for Every Age

A box only works if everything fits on the night, so size carefully and early:

  • Use each product page's size chart. Child, women's and men's sizes are listed separately, so measure each person instead of guessing from age.
  • Compare to pajamas that fit well now. Chest, waist and length measurements are easiest to check against a favorite pair at home.
  • Leave room for growth only if you can. Kids who are between sizes may swim in a bigger pair, which can be fine for sleeping but looks loose in photos.
  • Check the whole group at once. Write everyone's size on one list before you order so nobody is missed.

Box Ideas by Age

  • Toddlers. Pajamas, a sturdy board book, a small stuffed toy and a sippy cup of warm milk. Keep pieces large and safe.
  • Kids. Pajamas, a picture book, hot cocoa supplies and a movie or game card for the family to play together.
  • Tweens and teens. Pajamas they actually like (let them choose the print), a paperback, a favorite snack and a playlist for the evening.
  • Adults. Pajamas, a candle or a mug, a treat, and a short note. Parents often skip their own box, so make one anyway.

One shared box for the whole family also works. Put each person's pajamas in a folded stack with their name on top, then add the book, the treat and the activity in the middle.

How to Plan the Reveal and the Photo

  • Pick a time. After dinner and before bedtime gives enough time to change, read and take pictures without anyone getting tired.
  • Change first, photograph second. Everyone puts the pajamas on, then the family gathers by the tree. The pajamas are the photo outfit, so there is no second outfit to plan.
  • Light the room softly. Tree lights and a lamp are enough. Turn off harsh overhead lights.
  • Use a tripod or a timer. That way the parent who usually takes the photos is in the picture too.
  • Keep the shots quick. A few frames of the group, then candid ones during the book or movie. Children stay patient when the posing is short.

Timing: Order Early for a Fixed Date

Christmas Eve does not move, so the pajamas need to be in your hands with time to spare. Each product page shows its estimated delivery window, so check it before you order, and order early enough to try each size on and fix any mismatch with margin to spare. Wash and dry the pajamas once before the night, following the care label sewn into each garment, so they feel soft when they come out of the box.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do you put in a Christmas Eve box?

A simple version holds matching pajamas, a book, a treat and an activity such as a movie or a game. Keep it to four items so Christmas morning stays the focus.

Do the pajamas have to match exactly?

No. One print for everyone gives the strongest photo, but two prints in a shared color palette look coordinated and let people choose a pair they like.

How do I size pajamas for the whole family?

Use the size chart on each product page and measure each person separately. Child, women's and men's sizes are listed on their own, so compare chest, waist and length instead of guessing by age.

When should we open the Christmas Eve box?

After dinner and before bedtime works for most families. Everyone changes, the family takes a few quick photos by the tree, and the book and treat fill the rest of the evening.
