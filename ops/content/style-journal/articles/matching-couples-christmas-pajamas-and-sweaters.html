The easiest way to match as a couple at Christmas is to agree on one color family and one style, then let each person choose the fit that feels comfortable. Matching pajamas suit a cozy night in and Christmas morning, while matching sweaters suit parties, dinners, and holiday photos. This guide walks through how to choose either one, how to keep the look from feeling like a costume, and how to size a pair so both of you actually want to wear it.

Pajamas or Sweaters: Which Should You Choose?

Ask where you will wear the outfit most. If the answer is "on the couch with hot chocolate" or "Christmas morning," start with Christmas pajamas. If the answer is a party, a family dinner, or a photo, start with Christmas sweaters. Many couples pick both, using pajamas for the quiet moments and a sweater pair for anything with an audience.

You can also browse the wider couples collection for matching tops and coordinated sets that work well beyond December. If you plan to add kids later in the season, choosing a design that also comes in family sizes keeps your options open.

Three Ways to Match Without Matching Exactly

1. The identical pair

The same print, the same color, in each person's size. This is the classic look and it photographs beautifully. It is also the safest choice if you want a clear "we planned this" moment for a holiday card or a social post.

2. The coordinated pair

Two different pieces that share a palette. One partner might wear a plaid pajama top while the other wears a solid one in a matching shade. This works well when one of you prefers a quieter style. The link between the two outfits is the color, not the pattern.

3. The hidden-detail pair

Two mostly neutral pieces that share one small detail, such as a tiny embroidered motif or a cuff color. Couples who don't love loud holiday prints often like this approach best because it looks stylish at a party and still reads as a matching pair up close.

Color Palettes That Work for Two

  • Classic red and green. Bold, familiar, and easy to find in both pajamas and sweaters.
  • Cream and brown. Warm neutrals that look elegant on camera and pair well with wood tones and candlelight.
  • Navy and white. A cooler, calmer palette that suits snowy backdrops.
  • Buffalo plaid. A red or green check gives you a shared pattern without needing identical garments.
  • Black and gold. Best for evening events, especially if you want a dressier holiday party look.

If you are unsure, hold both pieces up together in daylight. Colors that look close under store lighting can look very different in a home photo, so check that the shades read as a pair in natural light.

How to Size a Matching Pair

Size each person separately. Matching sets are sold by size, so there is no reason for both of you to guess. Open the size chart on the product page, compare it with a pajama top or sweater that already fits you well, and choose from there. Measurements on size charts are approximate, so if you are between sizes, think about how you want the piece to feel: size up for a roomy, sleep-friendly fit, or stay true to size for a neater look at a party.

Fabric matters as much as size. Sweaters with a chunky knit run warmer, so consider how heated your gathering will be. Pajama sets in soft, stretchy fabric are the most forgiving for a long lazy morning. Read the description on each product page for fabric and care details, and follow the care label once your pieces arrive.

Photo Tips for Couples

  • Shoot near a window in the late morning for soft, even light.
  • Keep the background simple: a tree, a fireplace, or a plain wall lets the matching outfits stand out.
  • Try three poses: a hug facing the camera, a side-by-side laugh, and a candid shot holding mugs. The candid one is often the favorite.
  • Use a tripod or phone stand with a timer instead of asking a stranger. You will both look more relaxed.
  • Iron or steam sweaters first so the print or pattern lies flat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should couples match exactly or just coordinate?

Either works. Exact matches photograph as a clear pair, while coordinated colors feel more relaxed. Choose the one you'll both be comfortable wearing for several hours.

Can I mix a sweater and pajamas in the same color?

Yes. Matching the color family across two different garments is a simple way to look coordinated when one of you prefers a sweater and the other prefers pajamas.

How do I choose sizes for two people?

Use the size chart on each product page and size each person separately. Compare the chart with a piece that already fits well, and decide whether you want a relaxed or fitted feel.

What if we want to add kids later?

Look for designs that also come in child sizes. The matching family Christmas outfits collection has options for the whole household, so you can start as a pair and expand the look.
