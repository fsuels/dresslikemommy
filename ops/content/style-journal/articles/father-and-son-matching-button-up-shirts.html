The easiest way to match a father and son in button-up shirts is to choose one shared print, buy each size separately, and let the shirt do the talking with simple pants. A palm print, a small floral, or a tropical pattern reads as a pair right away, and a collared button-up looks dressed up without being stiff. This guide covers which prints work, how to size a dad and a growing boy, how to style the shirts for different occasions, and a few photo tips.

Why Button-Up Shirts Make a Great Father and Son Match

A matching tee is casual and easy, but a button-up gives a pair a little more polish. It works open over a plain tee, buttoned up for dinner, or with the sleeves rolled for a warm day. It also looks good in photos because the collar and the print frame the face.

Picking a Print You Will Both Wear

Palm and tropical prints

The most popular father and son match. A black and white palm print feels sharp and works for almost any occasion, while a softer palm print with a pink and black palette is playful. The matching Hawaiian outfits collection is the best place to browse tropical designs, including sets that also include shirts for mom and siblings.

Floral prints

A small daisy or floral print in green or another muted color feels fresh and slightly dressier. It suits a garden party, a family photo, or a spring and summer celebration.

Solids and subtle patterns

If dad prefers a quieter look, choose a shirt with a small pattern and let the child wear the same design. From a distance the two read as a pair without looking loud.

Whichever you choose, hold the print up next to what each of you already wears. A busy print looks best with plain bottoms such as khaki, navy, white, or denim.

How to Size Dad and Son

Size each person on their own, not as a set. Open the size chart on the product page, compare it with a shirt that already fits well, and choose from the chest and length measurements. Several of our button-up designs list sizes from Child 1 year up to Father 3XL, so one design can cover a toddler, an older boy, and dad. Check the size list on the exact product you like, since not every design is offered in every size.

For a growing boy, sizing up gives room to grow, and the sleeves can be rolled for now. Staying true to size gives a neater look for a photo. Age labels are only a guide, so use the measurements rather than the age. For more on sizing a whole household, see the matching outfit sizing guide.

How to Style the Shirts

  • Dinner or party: button the shirt, add dark chinos or jeans, and finish with clean shoes.
  • Vacation or beach day: wear it open over a white tee with shorts. Our matching family vacation outfits are worth a look if you want mom and siblings to join in.
  • Family photos: keep the bottoms plain so the print stands out, and add shirts for the rest of the family from the family tops collection.
  • Everyday wear: roll the sleeves and leave the top button undone for a relaxed look.

Easy Photo Ideas for Dads and Sons

  • Stand side by side and cross your arms in the same way. It shows off the match immediately.
  • Try a walking shot, hand in hand, from behind or from the side.
  • Crouch to your son's eye level for a close-up where the collars and prints show.
  • Take photos in the morning or late afternoon for soft light.
  • Steam or iron both shirts first so the print lies flat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should the father and son wear the exact same shirt?

Identical shirts look like a clear pair, and they are the simplest choice. If you prefer a subtler match, choose two shirts in the same color family with different patterns.

Are button-up shirts comfortable for kids?

Choose a size that gives room in the chest and shoulders, and check the fabric details on the product page. Rolling the sleeves and leaving the top button open keeps a child comfortable.

What do we wear on the bottom?

Keep it plain: khaki, navy, white, black, or denim. A busy print looks best with solid bottoms.

Can mom and siblings match too?

Yes. Browse the matching Hawaiian outfits and family tops for designs that cover the whole household.
