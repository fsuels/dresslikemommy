# Dress Like Mommy Growth North Star

Purpose: define the destination so every continuation prompt and subagent workstream moves toward the same business outcome.

## Owner's Goal In Plain English

The owner wants Dress Like Mommy to make more money, not just get more traffic.

The set goal is to build and run a profitable paid-growth machine for Dress Like Mommy: Google Ads and Pinterest campaigns active across every viable language/market, executed expertly and safely, aiming for as many profitable conversions as possible at about `650% ROAS`.

This is not an audit project. Audits, packets, trackers, and readbacks exist only to support action that can make sales happen: building controlled campaigns, enabling exact-approved tests, improving targeting/copy/assets/landing pages, removing blockers, monitoring performance, adding evidence-backed negatives, scaling winners, and killing waste.

The goal is aggressive but intelligent growth:

- Increase sales.
- Increase profit.
- Maintain or move toward about `650% ROAS`.
- Avoid expensive wasted clicks.
- Use Google Ads, Pinterest, Merchant Center, Shopify, website CRO, tracking, feed quality, languages, and international markets together.
- Use subagents in parallel where possible so work moves fast without conflict.

## What The Owner Has Told Us

- The site is `dresslikemommy.com`.
- The business sells matching family / mommy-and-me / daddy-and-me / vacation / photo-day / pajama / swimwear styles.
- The owner clarified on 2026-05-07 that Dress Like Mommy is a dropshipping business with no physical store and no owned physical inventory. Growth work must not imply a retail location, warehouse, local inventory, stocked inventory, or guaranteed on-hand stock; platform availability/inventory labels are channel salability diagnostics only.
- The owner wants the AI to act like the responsible marketing operator, not just an advisor.
- The owner wants actual implementation, not slow endless audits.
- The owner wants visible progress in actual sales-moving changes, not just documentation that something is blocked.
- The owner expects the agent to keep moving until every safe local/read-only/paused/approved live lane is advanced, and every remaining blocker has an exact next unblock action.
- The owner wants low-cost qualified traffic and strong conversion quality.
- The owner thinks about economics clearly: if AOV is around `$70` and gross margin is around `50%`, then ad spend must stay tight enough that marketing plus returns still leaves profit.
- The owner considers around `$0.25` per click expensive in many cases and prefers CPC near or below `$0.20` when testing.
- The owner wants to exploit international opportunity because the site has many languages and has received orders from places such as Denmark, the UK, and Switzerland.
- The owner does not want campaigns launched blindly; they want smart, controlled aggression.
- The owner wants subagents coordinated like an AI team, especially when using logged-in ChatGPT Atlas browser tabs for Shopify Admin, Google Ads, Merchant Center, GA4, Search Console, Pinterest, and related accounts.
- The owner has explicitly clarified that time is money: paid-growth sessions should not appear as one slow agent when an orchestrator plus subagents can move faster. The intended default is a parent/orchestrator that immediately splits safe parallel work into subagents, assigns each lane its own scope/tab/surface, keeps approvals centralized, and integrates results into one packet.
- The owner also explicitly clarified that one stuck lane must not stop the larger mission. If Merchant, a browser account, a policy review, or an approval-gated action is blocked, other agents should continue independent safe work that moves the business toward revenue/profit growth.

## What The Promise Land Looks Like

The destination is not "a lot of campaigns exist." The destination is a controlled paid-growth machine.

A strong reached-state looks like this:

0. Work is judged by sales-moving progress.
   - Campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, product groups, feeds, landing pages, and tracking surfaces are moved forward when safe and approved.
   - Audits are not considered progress unless they directly produce an action, approval packet, blocker removal, optimization decision, or verified performance readback.
   - Each session should end closer to revenue: active/eligible campaigns monitored, paused-ready campaigns built, copy/assets improved, landing pages fixed, catalog blockers removed, negatives/keywords refined, or exact approval gates narrowed.

1. Measurement is trusted.
   - Purchase conversion is primary.
   - Purchase value is dynamic.
   - Currency and transaction IDs are correct.
   - Micro-conversions do not inflate ROAS.
   - Pinterest and Google events are good enough to optimize.

2. Feed and catalog are healthy.
   - Paid products are approved and eligible.
   - Product attributes needed for apparel are present: age_group, gender, color, size, item_group_id, product_type, Google product category where possible.
   - Merchant issues affecting the paid cohort are fixed or isolated.
   - Product scope is intentional; low-quality, low-margin, or risky products are excluded from paid tests.

3. Website traffic is conversion-ready.
   - Paid landing pages are visually clean on mobile and desktop.
   - Shipping, delivery, returns, and "priced per piece / choose one size per person" are clear.
   - No broken collection grids or mixed-language trust killers on paid landing pages.
   - International pages used for ads are good enough for real shoppers.

4. Google Ads has controlled growth infrastructure.
   - Brand Search is governed and not wasting spend.
   - Standard Shopping remains tightly scoped and monitored.
   - Nonbrand Search campaigns exist for high-intent themes with exact/phrase keywords, tight negatives, and low CPC controls.
   - International Search tests are segmented by country/language so winners and losers are visible.
   - PMax is not enabled until feed, assets, conversion quality, product scope, and brand controls are clean.

5. Pinterest has controlled discovery infrastructure.
   - Official Pinterest app pixel/tag/CAPI path is clean.
   - Catalog is healthy.
   - US retargeting and catalog/product-group tests are built before broad international spend.
   - Creative is visual, seasonal, family-photo/vacation focused, and measured against real purchase quality.

6. Economics are explicit.
   - Campaigns have target CPA/CPC guardrails based on AOV, margin, expected conversion rate, and return risk.
   - Testing budgets are small enough to learn without burning cash.
   - Winners are scaled gradually.
   - Losers are paused quickly.

7. International growth is tested intelligently.
   - Markets are not ignored just because they are outside US/UK/CA/AU.
   - Markets are not launched blindly just because CPC may be cheap.
   - Each market has a readiness score: shipping clarity, landing language, currency/checkout, tracking, catalog eligibility, and expected economics.
   - Cheap CPC markets get controlled tests only when the page and checkout can convert.

8. Agents work as an organized team.
   - Parent agent owns approvals and final integration.
   - Subagents work on separate surfaces and tabs.
   - Parallel work is the default for paid-growth tasks when the tool environment supports it.
   - The parent keeps the critical path moving instead of waiting idly for sidecar research.
   - A blocker in one lane is routed around when other safe lanes can continue.
   - Work is documented with evidence packets and continuity anchors.
   - Each session ends with a continuation prompt that says what is done, what remains, and what the next best action is.

## Definition Of Done

The growth sprint is "done enough" when:

- Google Ads and Pinterest have active, expert, controlled campaign structures for every owner-approved viable market/language, or a documented reason why a market is excluded.
- Key campaigns are either live with approved low-risk budgets or paused-ready with exact approval gates.
- Merchant/feed blockers for the paid cohort are resolved or safely isolated.
- Conversion tracking is trusted for ROAS decisions.
- Paid landing pages for active markets are clean enough to convert.
- There is a weekly optimization routine using spend, CPC, conversion rate, CPA, ROAS, search terms, product performance, country performance, and return risk.
- The owner can look at reporting and know which markets/products/campaigns are making money, which are learning, and which should be killed.
- Every remaining blocker has at least two grounded recovery paths tried or ruled out, evidence, and one exact next unblock action.

## Not The Goal

- Not "get the most clicks."
- Not "launch every language."
- Not "turn on PMax because it is automated."
- Not "cheap traffic at any quality."
- Not "ROAS based on inflated micro-conversion value."
- Not "keep auditing forever without building."
- Not "write packets instead of making sales-moving changes."
- Not "stop because one platform or browser lane is blocked."

## Continuation Prompt Rule

Every session should end with a prompt that includes:

- Latest continuity anchor.
- What was completed.
- What remains blocked and why.
- Exact next approval gate if needed.
- Which subagents should run next and what tabs/surfaces they own.
- The current closest path to the North Star.
