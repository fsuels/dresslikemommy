## Decision Rules

- Every decision must name evidence.
- Every spend/status/budget/bid decision must name before-state and after-state readbacks.
- If evidence is stale, the decision can only be "read back", "hold", "prepare", or "request approval".
- When `spend_authorization.md` is `APPROVED_ACTIVE`, agents should not wait passively for the owner on green-gated bounded paid-media actions; they must monitor, diagnose, act inside caps when quality gates pass, read back, and log.
- `expert_growth_playbook_2026.md` is the strategy floor for 2026 paid-growth decisions; cheap traffic, broad automation, duplicate targeting, or passive monitoring must be rejected when it does not support profitable sales at about `650% ROAS`.
- Zero impressions after 24 hours requires same-day diagnosis and a long-tail/auction-entry decision; T+72 is an escalation point, not permission to wait.
- Owner's hard active Search CPC cap is `$0.15`. A row that requires more than `$0.15` for auction entry is not an action row; it is rejected or stays in validation.
