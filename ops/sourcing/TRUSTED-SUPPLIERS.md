# Trusted Supplier Scorecard

Updated every expansion round. Gate and tiers: `ops/sourcing/CONTINUOUS-EXPANSION-WORKFLOW.md` §3. All orders go to BuckyDrop's Huizhou Warehouse1 (Guangdong).

Tiers:
- **A (preferred):** passes every gate, with a proven track record.
- **B (watch/test):** passes the gate but has not been checked or proven for our categories.
- **Avoid:** failed the gate, or has incidents.

Readings are from the 1688 detail page on the date shown. Re-read them each round.

Suppliers whose offers promise 3/7/15-day dispatch no longer pass for new listings, even where the 48h pickup rate or our own lead times look fast.
