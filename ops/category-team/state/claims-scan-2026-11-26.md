## Method and limits
GraphQL read-only: products(status:active) 328 nodes with metafields(first:30) and media(first:30) alt; collections 54 nodes (published count by `resourcePublicationsCount`). Sentence-level pass with ~12 regexes per family, then a read of every distinct hit; false positives (e.g. 'hot' in 'photo', Google product category 'Sleepwear') were removed by hand. Not covered: translations, theme-only strings, blog, pages, policy pages, metaobject-reference metafields (fabric/color pattern names), variant titles, `custom_label_*` values other than the above (they are neutral labels: season, category, print name), and live rendered pages. Rows are exact English text; where the same sentence exists on several products the first handle is shown with '+N more'.
