# Reviews investigation, 2026-11-26 (read-only; no theme, product or commit changes)

## Facts
1. Review app: Judge.me ("judge-me-reviews"). Evidence: `config/settings_data.json` has the app embed `judgeme_core` enabled (disabled:false); `templates/product.json` has the `preview_badge` block (under the title) and a full-width `review_widget` apps section. No Loox, Yotpo, Stamped or Okendo anywhere. Collection and card star display is theme-native: `snippets/card-product.liquid` reads `reviews.rating`/`rating_count`, but `show_rating` is false in `templates/collection.json` and in the product template card settings, so cards never show stars.
2. Review data (admin API, read-only, counts only, no customer text): 328 ACTIVE products. Exactly 1 has a review count above zero: `leopard-mesh-mommy-and-me-swimsuits`, 1 review, 5.0 average (metafield updated 2026-03-02). Total real reviews on active products: 1. 284 active products carry `judgeme.widget` and `judgeme.badge` metafields, but every badge reads zero reviews except that same product. So the app is installed and has rendered widgets for most products, yet almost nothing has been collected.
3. Live pages (5 fetched 2 s apart: home, 3 sweater PDPs, 1 pajama PDP, plus the Christmas pajamas collection): Judge.me settings script/CSS and cdn.judge.me loader are present on every page. No rendered star badge, no review count, no review list and no `jdgm` widget container elements appear in the server HTML of any page (the widget either injects client-side or renders nothing for zero reviews; the badge CSS hides badges with average 0.00). No stars on collection cards. Product JSON-LD contains no `aggregateRating` on any page checked. `snippets/pdp-review-social-proof.liquid` is designed to show a "New arrival" pill instead of "No reviews" for zero-review products. Shoppers therefore see no review signal today, which is correct because there are no real reviews to show.
4. Review request emails: not visible from theme or public pages; depends on Judge.me settings (needs owner login). Judge.me core embed being on and the loader being live is consistent with requests being possible, but the 1 review in about 9 months of store life suggests requests are off, mistimed, or buyers are few. UNKNOWN until the owner checks the app.

## Recommendation, ranked by impact
1. Turn on post-delivery review requests in Judge.me (timing after the estimated delivery of 12-16 days, plus a reminder, photo reviews allowed). Owner step (app login). This is the only thing that creates real reviews.
2. Verify with the owner (same login) that the Judge.me sender email is verified and requests are not paused or in "test mode"; export the past 12 months of orders to send a one-time request to genuine past buyers (Judge.me "request reviews" for past orders; real buyers only, no import of third-party reviews). Owner step (app login).
3. Leave the theme as is for now. `aggregateRating` in the JSON-LD is already coded to appear only when a real count exists (reads `reviews.*` and `judgeme.*`); nothing to add. Theme change the CEO can release: none needed.
4. Once at least a few products have real reviews: set `show_rating` true on collection cards (the code already requires a real rating metafield; theme change the CEO can release). Not possible without real reviews.
5. Do not add review claims, star badges or "customers love" text on products without real counts. Not possible without real reviews.
