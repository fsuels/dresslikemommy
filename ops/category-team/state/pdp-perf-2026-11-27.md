## Proposals (not published)
1. Render the country list once: keep the drawer copy, have the desktop dropdown reuse it (or fetch it on open). Saves ~29 KB raw, ~900 DOM nodes. Risk medium: localization-form JS, two form ids, no-JS fallback.
2. Checkmark only on the active country (others get an empty span of the same width). Saves ~13 KB raw, 170 SVGs per selector copy. Risk low, but touches the selector look if the spacer width is wrong; needs a visual check of both dropdowns.
3. Drawer mega panels on demand (section rendering on first drawer open). Saves ~25-35 KB raw on every page. Risk medium-high: drawer links for crawlers/no-JS, open delay on slow phones.
Bigger than any of these: cart-drawer markup (99 KB with the test cart's lines in it; size for an empty cart not measured) and the 237 KB head (Shopify/app scripts).
