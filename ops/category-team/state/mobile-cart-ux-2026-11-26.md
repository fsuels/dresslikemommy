# Mobile cart UX, add -> cart -> checkout (2026-11-26, read-only)

Question: why only 14% of organic carts reach checkout (US desktop 43%, US mobile 17%, non-US 9-10%, RO 54->3->0, UK 17->0). Data: `cart-dropoff-2026-11-26.md`.

Method: own tab in the built-in browser pane, live www host, Android-Chrome emulation at 375x812 (mobile preset) and 1280x800. 18 page loads, at least 5 s apart, plus 6 `shipping_rates.json` calls; no 429, no challenge. Paths: US mobile Nordic Yoke PDP -> add -> drawer -> `/cart` -> checkout; US desktop Reindeer PDP -> add -> drawer; RO mobile `/ro/...?country=RO` PDP -> drawer -> `/ro/cart` -> checkout; GB mobile `?country=GB` Reindeer PDP -> drawer -> `/cart` -> checkout; `/ro/collections/christmas-sweaters`. Nothing typed into checkout; no form submitted. Test cart cleared (`/cart/clear.js`, 0 items), session back to US/USD/en, viewport reset to desktop, tab closed.
Code refs are to `origin/main` (= live theme, last sync 3f77af7). The local working tree is behind it: the delivery/Christmas snippets cited below do not exist in the local checkout yet.
Not repeated from today's `conversion-audit` and `mobile-walk`: price headline, mobile photo, 1.3-item drawer window, cart-page policy length, Italian Christmas line, "Translation missing", Czech formats.

## Bottom line
No hard blocker on the cart -> checkout step, on mobile or desktop, in the US, Romania or the UK. Mechanically everything works: the drawer opens in 0.66 s (mobile) / 0.76 s (desktop) after Add, with "Check out" visible without scrolling; checkout opens in about 1 s, without an intermediate page; it is translated (`ro-ro`, `en-gb`), the currency matches the cart (RON 435.60 = 435.60 lei; GBP 28.80 = 28.80 GBP), and shipping is free in both. So RO 54->3 and UK 17->0 are not caused by a missing shipping zone, a price jump or a broken button. The losses come from what shoppers see (or don't see) before they tap, from the store's checkout settings, and probably from carts that were never real purchase intent (see cause 3).

## Ranked causes

### 1. Non-US: the total cost is left open (tax/duty), and the shipping policy says import taxes are the buyer's problem. OWNER (then FIX_THEME wording)
- RO drawer and cart: "Taxele sunt calculate la finalizarea comenzii." (`snippets/cart-drawer.liquid:816` picks the "calculated at checkout" string because `cart.taxes_included` is false; same strings in `sections/main-cart-footer.liquid:12-94`). RO checkout cost summary: Subtotal 484,00 lei, WELCOME10 -48,40, Transport Gratuit, Total 435,60 lei, and **no tax line at all**. UK checkout: Subtotal £32.00, discount -£3.20, Free shipping, Total £28.80 GBP, **no VAT line**.
- The "Detalii despre livrare" panel on the RO cart (the store's own shipping policy, fetched in the cart page) says, translated: import taxes, VAT, clearance fees and customs duties charged by the destination country are the customer's responsibility unless checkout shows them as included. Checkout shows nothing. Result: a Romanian or UK shopper is told the courier may bill them at the door, for an amount nobody shows.
- Localized prices already sit about 15-18% above plain conversion (RO Nordic adult 285 RON vs 49.99 x 4.9406 = 247; child 216 vs 188; UK Reindeer adult £32.00, compare-at £38.00; RO variants have `compare_at_price: null`, so no sale badge). A shopper paying a VAT-sized premium and still warned about import VAT has a reason to stop.
- Same finding for CZ/CA/IT/DE/FR/ES/UK/AU in `cz-ca-funnel-2026-11-26.md` (cause 3) and `de-fr-es-uk-au-funnel-2026-11-26.md` (6). This walk adds RO and the first checkout screens of RO and UK.
- Fix: the owner chooses one of two options. (a) Collect EU VAT under IOSS and UK VAT (under £135), with prices shown "taxes included" (Shopify Settings > Taxes and duties; Markets). (b) Keep shipping without duties paid (DDU) and say so plainly. Then the theme changes one line in the drawer and the cart for non-US shoppers: "Prices include VAT, no fees on delivery" for (a), or one honest sentence for (b). Expected effect: largest on EU/UK, which is most of the 9-10% group. Risk: tax registration is a legal/money step, so only the owner can take it.

### 2. Checkout asks for a phone number. OWNER (Settings > Checkout)
- US, RO and UK checkout: `input[name=phone]` required (`required=true`, "Telefon" / "Phone"); the US and RO checkouts also show "Text me with news and offers" next to it. On a phone, the form is: email, country, first name, last name, address, apartment, postcode, city, county/state, **phone**, then card. The other audits found the same in IT, CZ and CA.
- Fix: set phone to Optional (Settings > Checkout > Customer information). Expected effect: a mobile-first form loses one field and one privacy worry; it lifts both mobile reach and completion. Risk: low; the carrier may lose a contact number, so keep phone optional on the shipping form only if BuckyDrop needs it. Shopify Analytics' "reached checkout" counts arrival, so this fix mostly moves checkout -> purchase (RO 3 -> 0, CA 9 -> 5), not cart -> checkout.

### 3. Cart counts outside the US look inflated, so part of the "drop" is not lost shoppers. NEEDS_DATA
- Add-to-cart sessions / sessions, organic 60 days: US 180/2768 = 6.5%; CA 15%; UK 14%; **RO 54/208 = 26%**; `/ro` Christmas collection entry 13/43 = 30%; `/ro` family pajamas entry 12/29 = 41%. Four times the US rate, but ending in 0 purchases, does not look like purchase intent. Either the cart is used to see a total or delivery (a price calculator), or adds come from something automated or unusual.
- Ruled out on this walk: quick-add on collection cards (the `/ro/collections/christmas-sweaters` mobile page has 25 cards, 0 add forms, 0 visible add buttons); an add on drawer open or variant change (the data file already checked this).
- Next data, Experto SEO, read-only: for RO and the UK, carts by `referrer_source`, by `landing_page_path`, by hour, and repeated cart sessions from the same city/day. Also compare `product_added_to_cart` against GA4 `add_to_cart` for the same days. Expected effect: it settles whether RO/UK need a fix at all, or whether "54 carts" is noise.

### 4. Mobile `/cart` page: Check out sits below the fold with no sticky bar; the "Complete the family" tile comes first. FIX_THEME
- US `/cart`, 1 item, 375x812: CHECK OUT at Y=1023 (1.26 screens). RO `/ro/cart`, 2 items: Y=1387 (1.7 screens). UK `/cart`, 2 items: Y=1346. Page height 3,255-3,619 px. No fixed or sticky element on the page (0 position:fixed nodes in view), so there is no sticky checkout bar. Order: items -> "Complete the family" tile (Y 429-665 US) -> "Have a discount code?" -> promo -> shipping -> delivery -> country checker -> total -> CHECK OUT -> wallets (Y 1085 US, 1408 UK) -> trust -> "Complete the look".
- Desktop sees the same order beside the items, so the button is near the first screen there. That is a real mobile-only difference, but small: the header cart icon opens the drawer, so only shoppers who land on `/cart` (cart reminder emails, links) meet it.
- Fix: on `/cart` under 750px, show a slim sticky bar at the bottom with "Estimated total + Check out" (reuse `#checkout` via `form="cart"`), or move the totals + CHECK OUT block above the family tile (`sections/main-cart-footer.liquid` cart__ctas at line 276; tile rendered from `snippets/dlm-complete-family.liquid`). Expected effect: small-moderate on the mobile `/cart` share. Risk: low; keep the sticky bar off when the drawer is open.

## US desktop 43% vs US mobile 17%: what differs
- Same theme flow on both: drawer opens after Add (mobile 0.66 s, desktop 0.76 s), Check out at 642 (mobile) / 648 (desktop) px from the top, same wallets, same `/checkout` redirect (1 redirect, page interactive at about 1.0 s on mobile).
- Mobile-only differences measured: the PDP Add button is at Y=1658 (2 screens) vs Y=1185 on desktop (already in conversion-audit 2); the `/cart` page button is below the fold with no sticky bar (cause 4); the checkout form is long on a phone and asks for a phone number (cause 2).
- Nothing in the cart path explains a 2.5x gap on its own. The direct-traffic data (desktop sessions reaching checkout without an add in the same session) suggests mobile carts are finished later on another device or in another session. Session ratios cannot show that: NEEDS_DATA (checkouts by customer across sessions; Shopify's "returning" is not usable, per the data file).

## Ruled out (with evidence)
- Shipping not offered or charged for RO/UK: `shipping_rates.json` returns one rate, "Free Standard Shipping" 0.00, for Romania (Bucharest 010011, county B) and for the UK (SW1A 1AA), and both checkouts show free shipping. Both countries are in the checkout country list.
- Price/currency jump between cart and checkout: RO cart 435,60 RON = checkout 435,60 lei; UK cart £28.80 = checkout £28.80 GBP.
- Untranslated or alarming first checkout screen: RO is fully Romanian (`lang="ro-RO"`, path `/ro-ro`, county list); UK English. Express area is blank for about 1 s while wallets load (RO: PayPal and Google Pay; UK: PayPal, Google Pay, Venmo; US: Shop Pay plus others). Card, PayPal and USDC are listed under Payment in all three.
- Required checkbox, terms or note before checkout: none on drawer or `/cart` (no required inputs, no note field).
- Anything covering the button, or anything that intercepts the click: no fixed overlays in view; the only checkout click listener on live main is analytics (`assets/analytics.js:1256-1270`), which does not stop navigation. Button 329x47 (drawer), 51 px high (cart page).
- Add-to-cart with no size: the inline prompts "Pick a size" / "Pick a color" appear and the page scrolls to them; the sticky bar ("SELECT SIZE") scrolls to the builder (`sections/main-product.liquid:2462-2471`). Clear.
- Quick-add on mobile collection cards inflating carts: none present.
- A test artifact, not a site bug: the UK checkout first opened in RON with Romania/Bucharest preselected, because the same cart token had been estimated for Romania earlier in this session; after a UK estimate it showed GBP/United Kingdom ("Currency updated for United Kingdom"). Real shoppers rarely switch countries mid-cart. Do not act on it.
