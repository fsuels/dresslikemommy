## Claims Avoided

Do not use these in ads, pins, assets, sitelinks, or descriptions unless a fresh live readback proves the exact claim:

- Fast shipping, free shipping, express shipping, guaranteed delivery, or delivery date promises.
- Discounts, promo codes, sales, markdowns, `Best Deals`, or limited-time offers.
- Review counts, star ratings, top-rated, customer-count, social-proof, or `trusted since` claims.
- Best seller, most popular, viral, trending, new weekly drops, or volume claims.
- Fabric/quality claims such as premium, luxury, quality fabrics, soft cotton, or durable unless product-level proof exists.
- `200+ styles`, broad catalog-size claims, or other inventory counts.
