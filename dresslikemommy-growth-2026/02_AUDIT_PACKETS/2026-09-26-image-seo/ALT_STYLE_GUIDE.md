# Dress Like Mommy — image alt text style guide (search engines + AI assistants)

Goal: each alt text tells Google Images, Google Shopping and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) exactly what is visible in THIS image, using the words a shopper would search, so the image can rank and be recommended. It is also read aloud to blind shoppers, so it must be natural English.

Rules
1. Describe what is actually visible in the image. Look at it. Never guess.
2. Length 60-125 characters (hard max 125, hard min 15). One sentence, no period needed.
3. Lead with who and what: people + garment. e.g. "Mother and daughter in matching royal blue halter swimsuits with paisley print bottoms on a beach"
4. Include, when visible: who (mother, daughter, father, son, baby, toddler, family of four, couple), garment type (dress, swimsuit, pajamas, sweater, tee, set), color, print/pattern, one or two notable details (halter neck, ruffle hem, puff sleeves, button front), and briefly the view or setting (front view, back view, close-up of the print, flat lay, beach, living room by a Christmas tree).
5. Use one natural shopping phrase where true: "matching", "mommy and me", "family matching", "daddy and me", "matching couples". Use the product's own print name when it matches what you see (e.g. "Jingle Bells Santa", "Nordic reindeer", "Boo stripe").
6. Every alt text inside the same product must be different. Differentiate by who is shown, the view, or the detail (e.g. "Close-up of the red and white Nordic reindeer knit pattern on the family sweater").
7. Size charts / measurement tables: "Size chart for the <short product name> with adult and child measurements" (say "in cm and inches" only if both are visible).
8. Do NOT: start with "Image of", "Photo of", "Picture of"; use the brand or website name; mention prices, sales, discounts, shipping, stock, "best seller", quality claims, or fabric/material unless the product title states it; mention AI, ChatGPT, suppliers, 1688; repeat any word more than twice; use quotes or emojis; number images ("photo 2").
9. Keep it truthful: this store is dropshipping. Never imply a store, warehouse, or stock.
10. If something is wrong with the image, still write the alt, and add a short "flag": image does not match the product title, visible supplier watermark/logo, Chinese text, other brand logo, very low quality, duplicate of another image, size chart that seems to belong to a different product. Otherwise flag is "".
