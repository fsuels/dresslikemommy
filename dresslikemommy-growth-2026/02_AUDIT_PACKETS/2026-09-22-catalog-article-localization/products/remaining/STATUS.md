## Independent review

All 230 body candidates and 1,669 short/option candidates passed bounded independent meaning review. The six final review receipts and all selected file hashes are bound by `prepared_remaining_checks.json` and `remaining_manifest.json`.

- Swedish body lane: 99 accepted; 25 held. Seventy-seven strict source checks, 21 existing comment-only differences and one existing relocated table were independently verified. The relocated table and remaining markup were checked separately; no numeric mismatch was waived.
- Russian body lane: 93 accepted; 23 held; two detector false positives retained. Two Sunshine/Raglan rows have no English prose gap but retain outdated source-table differences, so they are held. They are not counted as resolved no-change findings.
- Other body candidates: 38 accepted after independent review, including 18 Blue Daisy translations. Exact corrections removed English upper-limit phrases in 36 table cells, clarified one Russian high neckline, and repaired two Russian role/care passages. Original artifacts and earlier reviews remain preserved.
- Option/color candidates: 801 accepted across all 647 unique source/locale/value pairs. Three printed-text labels now retain the actual English garment words with Swedish meanings in parentheses.
- Title, type and metadata candidates: 868 accepted after all 759 original distinct meaning pairs were reviewed. Eight exact meaning fixes were applied. Two source-contradictory red-heart-print SEO fields were excluded because the bound title/body describes red cardigans with gold heart buttons.

Only final selected filenames in `remaining_manifest.json` should be used. Original versions of corrected candidates remain as review history and are superseded.

## Remaining holds and metadata

`remaining_hold_summary.json` indexes 918 held fields across 146 products: 527 meta descriptions, 258 bodies, 129 meta titles and four titles. Exact source/before evidence and the required source or measurement decision are linked per field. Holds do not apply to unrelated qualified translations on the same product.

The largest shared source issue is the shipping/return promise in meta descriptions. Other holds include shirt-versus-T-shirt or garment-construction contradictions, inconsistent material claims, internal drafting instructions exposed in source, malformed translated HTML, duplicate or changed charts, and inherited image differences. No source facts or measurements were silently rewritten.

`no_change_outdated_metadata.json` records 96 retained-language rows whose saved Shopify `outdated` flag is still true: 40 product types, 24 bodies, 16 meta titles and 16 titles. This packet does not claim to clear that metadata. The other nine original no-change rows and two additional Russian detector false positives also receive no mutation from this lane.

## Limits and next action

The parent can fresh-read and apply the exact qualified candidate package in guarded batches, capture mutation receipts and API after-state, then verify affected public pages. Candidate source snapshots are local evidence, not proof of current live freshness. Source changes require a new digest and renewed comparison; current-before changes require reconciliation rather than overwriting another writer.

This lane made only local candidate/review artifacts. It made no live API writes, provider calls, Git changes, English-source edits, price/stock/handle changes, or ad/negative changes. That is a statement about this lane, not a global account diff. Faithful translation and table equivalence do not independently substantiate supplier claims, measurement accuracy, or every untouched image attribute; this is not full-store native-language certification.
