## 1. Denmark: validate and strengthen the existing dress collection

**Query → URL:** “mor og datter kjole” (7 clicks/82 impressions), “mor datter kjole” (6/37) → https://www.dresslikemommy.com/da/collections/dresses. Page: 37 clicks/568 impressions versus 26/336. Danish homepage /da: 30 versus 9 clicks. Shopify Denmark: four orders/$444.73 net in 90 days.

**Current:** URL observed in GSC; Danish title/meta/body not captured. English collection HTTP200, title “Mommy and Me Dresses | Mother Daughter”; this does not prove Danish metadata.

**Proposed review copy:**

- Title: Kjoler til mor og datter | Dress Like Mommy
- Meta: Find matchende kjoler til mor og datter. Se styles til familiebilleder, fester og ferier, og tjek størrelser og mål på produktsiden, før du vælger.
- Intro: Find et match til mor og datter. Åbn en style, og tjek de enkelte dele, størrelser og mål, før du vælger.
- Internal link: from the existing dresses tile on https://www.dresslikemommy.com/da to /da/collections/dresses, anchor “Se matchende kjoler til mor og datter”.

**Reason/gates:** strongest overseas combination of demand and orders. Inspect current Danish copy and query/page join first; retain a good existing title/meta. Native review, actual collection composition, Denmark currency/checkout and size-chart readability are required. No new delivery/material claims.

**Success:** collection-to-PDP clicks, organic Denmark orders and contribution profit; GSC clicks/CTR diagnostically over the next comparable 28 days.

**Disproof:** the queries predominantly land elsewhere, existing equivalent links/copy already work, or Denmark checkout/measurement fails. Fix that dependency first.

## 4. Meow Star Garden: repair product identity in search copy

**Query → URL:** broad “mommy and me” (8/238) is a research lead only; no PDP query attribution proven. https://www.dresslikemommy.com/products/meow-star-garden-mommy-and-me-pajamas had one Shopify order/$75.98 net in 90 days.

**Observed:** HTTP200; title “Cat Meadow Mommy & Me Pajamas — Set | Dress Like Mommy”; H1 “Meow Star Garden Mommy and Me Pajamas — Short-Sleeve Set”. Meta claims soft cotton. Body discusses four-layer gauze and long pants: product/style/material consistency needs verification.

**Proposed review copy:**

- Title: Meow Star Garden Mommy and Me Pajamas | Dress Like Mommy
- Meta: Explore Meow Star Garden mommy and me pajamas. Review the product details and size options before choosing matching looks for mom and daughter.

**Reason/gates:** directly observed identity conflict. Match actual product/photos/variants before release; title/meta repair alone does not fix conflicting body content. Do not retain fiber, delivery, review, availability or popularity claims without proof.

**Success:** verified agreement across product identity, rendered title/H1/meta and delivered-product evidence; then qualified organic PDP visits and sales.

**Disproof:** actual product identity supports Cat Meadow instead; correct the naming decision before publishing.
