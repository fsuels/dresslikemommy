- **Consent divergence:** GA4 rejects unknown/denied analytics, falling back to marketing only when analytics is unknown (173–185); rejected checkout callbacks are not queued or replayed. Ads defaults to trusting Shopify’s permission gate when privacy is unavailable/unknown (13, 139–179). Crucially, `marketingAllowed=false` plus `analyticsProcessingAllowed=true` **allows** Ads firing (166–167), contradicting README:40 and May 18 packet:31. `saleOfDataAllowed` is recorded, not enforced. Installed Shopify privacy requirements remain unknown. Consent is checked before asynchronous storage work, not immediately before transport (GA4:333–353, 465–475; Ads:61–97); no explicit consent fields appear in the GA4 MP body.
- **Client/session attribution:** GA4 creates its own stored client/session fallback (222–265); payloads use these identifiers (333–353). No explicit `session_start`, Ads click-ID, or manual-campaign parameters are implemented; page URL/referrer are sent on page views (406–416). Therefore stable local IDs do not prove continuity with another tag’s session. An offline synthetic cookie `GA1.1.123456789.1700000000` yielded `123456789`: the parser drops the final component (230–235). Actual cookie availability and resulting attribution remain unverified. Ads captures `gclid`/`gbraid`/`wbraid` from allowed page-view URLs, stores them for 90 days, and reuses them at purchase (40–51, 295–365). Consent updates alone do not recapture a previously rejected landing URL or clear stored identifiers.
- **Transaction identity/dedup:** Both normalize a numeric suffix and fall back from order ID to checkout token (GA4:273–278, 475; Ads:60, 240–246). Synthetic `alpha001` and `different001` both become `001`, creating a conditional collision risk when order ID is absent. GA4 permits an empty transaction ID; Ads rejects it (85–91). Neither implements durable local purchase deduplication. Ads deliberately sends fetch plus an image backup (396–415); shared IDs do not establish cross-action deduplication or current primary/secondary configuration (README:85).
- **Missing-event/transport risk:** Purchase capture depends on browser `checkout_completed` callbacks (GA4:465–484; Ads:53–98). No persistent retry/backfill mechanism exists in these files. GA4 beacon acceptance or opaque fetch completion (375–400), and Ads dispatch logging, do not acknowledge analytics ingestion. Suppressed callbacks, consent timing, transport loss, and schema differences are hypotheses, not explanations of the current missing-order cohort. Diagnostics can expose transaction/fallback-token-derived values (GA4:359; Ads:389–390); retain sanitized findings only.
- **Value/refunds:** Both send checkout `totalPrice` with a USD fallback (GA4:470–478; Ads:56–60), not verified retained merchandise revenue. GA4 also sends tax/shipping separately. No refund or Ads adjustment implementation appears; README:89 explicitly excludes refunds. Gross captured purchases, retained sales, and profit therefore require separate reconciliation.
