## Buyer-inquiry response playbook

These are **response drafts**, not canned messages to send indiscriminately. Root reads the full conversation, verifies the exact current product or support route, personalizes the answer and sends only within current correspondence authority. No cold DM campaign, automatic reply bot or mass mentions. No link unless it answers the actual question.

| Incoming need | Suggested response | Operator check |
|---|---|---|
| General size uncertainty | “Which outfit are you considering? Share the product link and I can help you find the relevant size chart. Please leave personal details out of the reply.” | Inspect the exact product chart and units. Clarify whether its measurements are body or garment measurements if the page establishes that. Never infer size from age alone. |
| “Is this the whole family set?” | “The selected option is the key detail. Which product are you looking at? I'll check what that option includes before pointing you to the right pieces.” | Read the chosen role/piece and price; do not assume all catalog items follow the same bundle rule. |
| Arrival for an event | “I don't want to guess about an event date. Check the current delivery information for your destination before ordering. If anything is unclear, use the store's official support route to confirm.” | Verify the live support route before adding it; do not request an address publicly or promise a shipping date. |
| Looking for a coordinated look | “Start with one color or piece you already love, then list who still needs an outfit. Our family-photo guide can help you plan the rest.” | Share the verified guide only if it answers the question. If a link is added, use a new stable, non-PII reply content tag and record its exact destination. |
| Product availability, fabric or construction | “Share the product link and the option you're considering so I can check the details for that piece.” | Current product evidence is required. A purchasable storefront variant is not supplier fulfillment proof; report only what the evidence supports. |
| An existing order problem | “I'm sorry there's a problem with your order. Please keep order and contact details out of this public thread. The store's official support route is the right place to review them.” | Verify and add the current official support destination privately or publicly as appropriate to the existing inquiry and authority. No refunds, cancellations, billing actions or order claims by this content lane. |
| A critical but genuine experience | “Thank you for explaining what happened. I don't want to speculate about your order here. Please use the store's official support route so the details can be reviewed.” | Acknowledge the specific issue where safe; no blame, invented investigation, resolution promise or deletion merely for criticism. |

Do not paste an operator note, unfilled placeholder or unverified link into a public reply. If there is no reliable product answer, say what remains unclear and route the exact question for verification. An offered “I can help” is followed by a real evidence check, not an invented fit recommendation.

For brand listening, use a few relevant searches and read before participating. Topic research can be recorded as anonymized counts such as “two questions about separate pieces”; do not retain handles, children's identities, customer details or fabricated quotes. A real buyer question can inform a later post only without exposing the person or pretending permissioned customer proof exists.
