# Dress Like Mommy: 30-day organic X relaunch

Prepared September 11, 2026. **State: LOCAL_DRAFT.** The root task owns execution and current account evidence. This plan does not establish a live schedule, a published post, private analytics access, or a sales result.

## Objective and test

Make [@dresslikemommy](https://x.com/dresslikemommy) a current, recognizable source of useful outfit advice, and test whether that advice helps adult shoppers reach products and buy. Run a 30-day organic pilot from the first verified relaunch publication, with **$0 ad spend, $0 subscriptions, and no more than two operator hours a week**.

Working audience: English-speaking adult parents and family-photo or occasion shoppers, with the U.S. as the starting market hypothesis. Include fathers and adult gift buyers. Audience fit on X remains unproved; the historical Shopify percentages in the supplied report are not a fresh baseline.

The directional business target is **two distinct, paid, noncancelled orders with an identifiable X-tagged journey and positive supported contribution by Day 30**. It is a small experimental target, not a forecast or a threshold for statistical certainty. A meaningful secondary signal is at least 20 non-operator sessions that reach a product page from the X cohort. Orders with missing costs or unresolved reversals remain provisional.

**Decision-critical premise:** practical outfit-planning content can attract shoppers rather than only social engagement. The falsifier is repeated relevant publication that produces no buyer questions or product progression, or visits that do not produce economically useful orders.

**Credible alternative:** maintain a small service presence on X and prioritize the existing article/Pinterest/organic-search lanes within their own ownership. X must earn additional time. It does not inherit the paid program's ROAS target or authority; with no ad spend, ROAS is undefined.

## Decision rules: hypotheses, not platform benchmarks

Use the first applicable rule at each checkpoint. Record the evidence and one next action; do not run all branches at once.

| Checkpoint / observed condition | Decision |
|---|---|
| At any time: destination is broken, exact content becomes misleading, or the account/receipt is uncertain | Hold affected queued pieces. Root resolves the exact fault, reads back the result and resumes only affected work. Preserve evidence; deleting a post cannot undo prior impressions. |
| Day 7: no distribution is visible after at least 24 hours, or post publication/readability is uncertain | Verify the exact public posts and links once, then adjust one relevant topic/conversation route within organic scope. Do not interpret zero views as a need to buy reach. |
| Day 14: eight intended slots are resolved, fewer than 10 attributable non-QA landing sessions and no genuine buyer questions | Change one hook or topic in week 3 and lower output to three posts. Do not increase spend or operator time. This is an audience/message test, not proof of a checkout fault. |
| Day 14 or later: at least 20 attributable non-QA landing sessions but no supported product progression | Inspect the guide-to-product step in the observed market/device before sending more link-led traffic. Prepare one clearer CTA or already-verified relevant destination; no storefront write is implied. |
| Day 30: at least 40 supported product-visiting sessions but zero confirmed orders | Stop expanding the acquisition cadence; route the product/offer/journey evidence to its existing owner. Continue only minimal inquiry coverage until a bounded correction is justified. |
| Day 30: at least two distinct confirmed orders, positive supported contribution, useful repeat questions/visits, and no unresolved customer-truth problem | Continue the better topic for another bounded cycle at the same or lower time ceiling. Reconcile labor and later reversals before calling it profitable; two orders do not justify paid scale. |
| Day 30: data is too sparse, costs are unknown, or no branch supports extension | Label the sales result inconclusive and reduce X to one useful post plus inquiry review within 30 minutes/week. Preserve the account and prioritize another already-owned organic lane. |

One high-view post cannot pass the continuation rule by itself. The small thresholds limit effort; they do not establish causal lift. Any future spend, paid feature, creator compensation or broader automation is a separate business decision with its own scope.
