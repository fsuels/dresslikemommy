1. **Verified amount contract:** `checkout_completed` sends `ea=purchase`, `gv=checkout.subtotalPrice.amount`, and its currency as `gc`. These are legitimate raw Microsoft fields; their names are not a defect. This records merchandise subtotal, excluding duties, shipping and taxes, rather than the customer's full charge. Product IDs are present; product quantities, line prices and transaction identity are absent. Microsoft marks `transaction_id` and `items` optional, so absence alone does not establish why conversions are missing. [Microsoft fields](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_uet_parameters_table), [Shopify checkout schema](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/web-pixels-api/standard-events/checkout_completed).

2. **Verified cookie API misuse:** the app passes `msclkid; expires=<date>` as a cookie name and the click ID as its second argument. Shopify documents either a bare name/value pair or one complete cookie assignment. Expiry must be part of the latter. The app also stores the ID in localStorage for 90 days and restores cookie-or-localStorage values; therefore this defect does **not prove** real attribution loss. Full click continuity requires a genuine ad-to-checkout readback. [Shopify cookie API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/web-pixels-api/standard-api/browser).

3. **Unproved production risks:** there is no explicit order/event deduplication, `asc`, or privacy-change subscription in this bundle. It also dereferences nullable `subtotalPrice` without validation. Shopify normally emits checkout completion once, can omit it if the triggering page fails, and gates app loading by declared privacy purposes. Neither missing source fields nor a healthy aggregate receiver badge proves exact purchase consent/delivery. Verify purchase and storefront senders separately across grant/refusal/revocation. [Shopify privacy](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/web-pixels-api/pixel-privacy), [Microsoft consent diagnostics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_uet_consent).
