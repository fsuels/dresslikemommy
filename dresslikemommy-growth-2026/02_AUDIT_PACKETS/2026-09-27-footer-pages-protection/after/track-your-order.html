Understanding tracking updates

My tracking says "not found" or "no information yet"

Tracking numbers are created before the carrier scans the parcel. It can take 24–48 hours, sometimes a few days on international routes, for the first scan to appear.

Tracking hasn't updated for several days

That's normal while a parcel flies between countries or clears customs. If there's no movement for 15 business days, or your order hasn't arrived within 30 days after it shipped, email us your order number and we'll investigate with the carrier.

The tracking number changed or shows a new carrier

International parcels are handed to a local carrier (for example USPS, Canada Post or Royal Mail) for final delivery. 17TRACK shows both carriers under the same number.

It says delivered, but I don't have it

Check around your property, with neighbors and building reception, and allow 48 hours because carriers sometimes scan early. Then email us within 14 days of the delivery scan and we'll open a claim with the carrier.

I received only part of my order

Orders with several items may ship in more than one parcel. Check your emails for a second tracking number. If you still have a missing item, write to us.
