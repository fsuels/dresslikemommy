Delivery times

Checkout shows the shipping method for your address before you pay. If checkout shows a delivery estimate, it is an estimate, not a guaranteed date. Customs, weather, holidays, carrier volume and local conditions can add time, especially from November through January.

Ordering for a date? For Christmas mornings, birthdays, weddings or photo sessions, order early and allow extra time. If you need an order by a specific date, email us before ordering and we'll tell you honestly whether it is realistic.

How your order travels

  1. Order confirmed: you receive a confirmation email.
  2. Processing (1–3 business days): your items are checked and packed. Busy seasons can add 1–2 business days.
  3. Shipped: we email your tracking number. Tracking can take 24–48 hours, sometimes longer on international routes, to show the first scan.
  4. In transit: follow your parcel on our Track your order page.
  5. Delivered: to the address you entered at checkout.

Orders with several items may arrive in more than one parcel, on different days. Tracking is emailed for each parcel when available.

Where we ship

We ship to the countries and regions shown at checkout. If checkout shows a shipping method for your address, we can deliver there. If your country isn't listed, email info@dresslikemommy.com before ordering. P.O. Box, APO/FPO and freight-forwarder addresses depend on carrier options and may not be supported.

Customs, duties and import taxes

Unless checkout shows that duties and taxes are included, import duties, taxes, brokerage or customs fees charged by your country are paid by the customer. We can't predict these charges, mark orders as gifts, or declare a lower value, because false declarations are illegal. If a parcel is refused, or never collected because of these charges, we can refund the items after the parcel returns to us, less the return shipping cost we were charged, which we will tell you, except where the law requires a full refund.

Please double-check your address

We ship to the address entered at checkout. Email us within 24 hours if it needs to change; once an order has shipped, we can't redirect it. If a parcel is returned or lost because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we'll help, but reshipping costs are the customer's responsibility.

Late, lost or "delivered" but not received

  • Tracking not moving? International parcels often go days without an update while clearing customs. If tracking shows no movement for 15 business days, or your order hasn't arrived within 30 days after it shipped, email us your order number. We'll open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, reship or refund it.
  • Marked delivered but not there? Check around your property, with neighbors and building reception, and wait 48 hours, because carriers sometimes scan early. Then contact us within 14 days of the delivery scan and we'll file a claim with the carrier and help you get it resolved.
  • Arrived damaged? Take photos of the parcel and the item and email us within 7 days of delivery. See our Returns & exchanges page.
