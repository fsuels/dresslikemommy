We want your whole family to love their matching outfits. This Refund Policy explains how returns, exchanges, refunds and cancellations work for orders from Dress Like Mommy, operated by FKG Trading LLC. A step-by-step version is on our Returns & exchanges page.

Return window

You may request a return or exchange within 30 days of delivery.

How to return

  1. Email info@dresslikemommy.com with your order number, the item(s) and the reason for the return.
  2. We usually reply within 1 business day. If a return is needed, we send a return authorization and the correct return address.
  3. Ship the item(s) within 14 days of our authorization, using a tracked service, and send us the tracking number.
  4. After the return arrives and passes inspection, we email you and issue your refund.

Returns must be authorized before shipping. Our business mailing address is not a returns center. Parcels sent without authorization or to an address other than the one we provide may be lost or delayed and may not be eligible for a refund.

Eligibility

To be eligible, items must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed and in original condition
  • Returned with all original tags attached and in their original packaging
  • Free of stains, pet hair, perfume, deodorant or other signs of use

Non-returnable items

  • Swimwear and intimates once the hygiene liner or seal has been removed or worn
  • Items marked "Final Sale"
  • Gift cards
  • Items requested for return more than 30 days after delivery

These exclusions do not apply to items that are damaged, defective or not as described.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item, the size or care label, and the packaging. Once the issue is confirmed, we will send a replacement or refund the affected item. In most cases you do not need to return it. Reports made after 7 days are still reviewed where the law gives you longer rights.

Exchanges

To change a size or color, email us with your order number. We usually place the new size as a new order and refund the original item once its return is approved.

Return shipping costs

  • We pay return shipping when we made an error or the item is damaged or defective.
  • For other returns, including change of mind and size exchanges, the customer pays return shipping. Use a tracked service: we cannot refund returns lost in transit without tracking.
  • When return postage would cost more than the item, we may offer a partial refund or store credit and let you keep the item instead. You can always choose a standard return.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank may take a further 2–5 business days to post it.
  • Standard shipping is included in our product prices, so no separate standard shipping charge applies. Paid express upgrades are refunded when we made an error or where the law requires it.
  • Items returned used, washed, damaged, altered or without tags may be refused and sent back to you at your cost, or receive a partial refund reflecting their reduced value, where the law allows.
  • Refunds for orders paid with a discount reflect the amount actually paid.
  • If you have not received an approved refund within 10 business days, check with your bank or card issuer, then email us and we will investigate.

Cancellations

Email us as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours of ordering. If the order has not entered fulfillment, we cancel it and refund you in full. Once an order has entered fulfillment it can't be cancelled; you can return it after delivery under this policy.

Refused, undeliverable or uncollected parcels

If a parcel returns to us because the address was incorrect or incomplete, or because it was refused or not collected (including refusal to pay import charges), we refund the items once the parcel is back with us, less the return shipping cost we were charged, which we will tell you, except where the law requires a full refund. Alternatively, we can reship it at your cost.

Payment disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute or chargeback. We usually answer within 1 business day and can often solve the problem faster. If a dispute is opened, we will share order, tracking and communication records with the payment provider.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit rights you have under consumer protection laws that cannot be excluded. For example, consumers in the EU and UK may cancel an online purchase within 14 days of receiving it without giving a reason. Tell us within that period, then return the items within 14 days. We refund the price and the cost of standard delivery within 14 days of receiving the items back or proof of return. Direct return costs are paid by the customer.

To use this right you can email us a clear statement, or use this model form: "To FKG Trading LLC (Dress Like Mommy), info@dresslikemommy.com: I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract for the sale of the following goods: [items], ordered on [date] / received on [date], order number [number], name, address, date."

Consumers in Australia keep their rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including remedies for items with a major failure.
