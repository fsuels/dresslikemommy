  • Process, fulfill and deliver your orders, including sharing shipping details with our fulfillment partners and carriers

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, some U.S. state privacy laws treat sharing browsing data with advertising partners for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out at any time; see section 7.

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember your language and currency, understand traffic, and support advertising. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some site features may then stop working.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

All customers can ask to access, correct or delete their personal information, and can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email.

  • U.S. state privacy rights (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others): you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete your information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and targeted advertising. Use our Do not sell or share my personal information page. Where required, a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser is treated as an opt-out request, as described on that page. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you also have the right to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you (orders), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention and improving our store), your consent (marketing emails and non-essential cookies) and legal obligations.

To make a request, email info@dresslikemommy.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we respond within the time required by law (usually within 30 days). An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization.

Newsletter discount (notice of financial incentive)

When you join our newsletter, we offer a one-time discount on your first order in exchange for your email address. We use it to send marketing emails. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.
