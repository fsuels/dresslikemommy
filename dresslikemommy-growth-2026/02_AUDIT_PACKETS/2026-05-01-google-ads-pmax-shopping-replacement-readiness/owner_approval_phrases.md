# Owner Approval Phrases

These phrases are intentionally narrow. They do not approve activation unless the phrase says enable.

## Paused Replacement Draft

`APPROVE CREATE PAUSED PMAX SHOPPING REPLACEMENT DRAFT ONLY; USE MERCHANT 124884876; KEEP ALL PMAX CAMPAIGNS PAUSED; DO NOT CHANGE STANDARD SHOPPING, BUDGETS, PRODUCT SCOPE, OR CONVERSION GOALS`

Allowed by that phrase:
- Create a new paused replacement PMax draft/shell.
- Use Merchant Center `124884876 - Dresslikemommy`.
- Keep PMax paused.
- Run readbacks and document blockers.

Not allowed by that phrase:
- Enable PMax.
- Edit Standard Shopping.
- Increase budgets.
- Expand product scope.
- Change conversion goals.
- Upload Customer Match/PII.

## Existing Bad PMax Archive/Rename

`APPROVE RENAME EXISTING PMAX SHOPPING UNITED STATES AS DO NOT USE WRONG MERCHANT; KEEP IT PAUSED`

Allowed by that phrase:
- Rename the existing bad PMax campaign for safety.
- Keep campaign paused.

Not allowed by that phrase:
- Enable, delete, or rebuild the existing campaign in place.

## Final Activation

`APPROVE ENABLE DLM_US_PMAX_PAID_READY_REPLACEMENT_DRAFT AT [BUDGET]/DAY NOW; KEEP STANDARD SHOPPING DECISION AS DOCUMENTED; DO NOT CHANGE CONVERSION GOALS`

Use only after:
- Fresh Merchant Center readback passes.
- Product scope is exact and approved.
- Standard Shopping overlap/cannibalization decision is documented.
- Final URL expansion/allowlist is captured.
- Brand posture is captured.
- Conversion-value readback passes.
- Asset and audience checks pass.
