Use the reviewed economics, for the same attributed cohort and reporting currency:

- `R` = retained merchandise after discounts/refunds + retained shipping, excluding tax.
- `V` = disjoint actual variable costs, net of evidenced recoveries; `H` = allocated overhead/other costs outside V; `CM = R − V`.
- All-in profit `P = R − V − H − A`; require `P/R ≥ 0.30` and business ROAS `R/A ≥ 6.5`.
- Therefore `A ≤ min(R/6.5, CM − H − 0.30R, authorized cash/loss allowance)`. Unknown inputs or nonpositive capacity yield no qualified spend. No speculative LTV.

For per-order allowable CPA `C` and actual CPC `c`, required conversion rate is `c/C`; capped CPC does not guarantee that rate.
