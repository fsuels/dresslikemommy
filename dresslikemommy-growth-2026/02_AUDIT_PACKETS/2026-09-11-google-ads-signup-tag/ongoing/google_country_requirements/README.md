## Findings that change a decision

**Russia is a paid-serving hold.** RU appears in Merchant's general country table, but current Google Shopping policy and Ads help explicitly retain the pause on ads serving to users in Russia. The RU row therefore records the paid pause for Shopping and Search separately from nominal country listing. Russian-language content for users elsewhere is not the same restriction. This evidence does not establish a free-listing ban or that the store currently targets RU. Root should check the actual campaign geography before any further paid-country decision. [Shopping policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6149970?hl=en), [Ads confirmation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9427120?hl=en).

**The opened tables do not support a claim that all 65 countries are approved Shopping targets.** The general table includes 26 of the store's codes, including RU; the separate issue-resolution table explicitly adds **SV as beta**, with account participation still unverified. Nine more appear in the Shopify app's sync table but not the opened general Shopping tables: **BG, CY, HR, LT, LU, LV, MT, RS, SI**. Their status is **UNRESOLVED documentation scope difference**, not unsupported. The remaining **29 codes** are not in those opened tables and remain **UNRESOLVED target support**. An expanded official search snippet differed from the page actually opened; it was not used to certify a target. [Country/currency table](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/160637?hl=en), [country issue and beta list](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12472394?hl=en), [beta limits](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7101265?hl=en), [app sync table](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13693394?hl=en).

**Do not replace territory codes with metropolitan parents.** Google explicitly limits France's Shopping coverage to metropolitan France. The mapping retains BL, GF, GP, MF, MQ, PM, RE, TF, WF and YT separately and flags that the FR target cannot substitute for their own support checks. AD, AX, UM and other separately coded locations likewise receive no inferred parent-country coverage. Absence from these Merchant tables does not establish a Google Ads Search prohibition or change Shopify shipping availability. [France exception](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/160637?hl=en).

**NO/EUR and MX/USD are potential currency-conversion paths, not proven currency errors.** Google permits automatic currency conversion while the feed, submitted shipping prices and buyer flow retain consistent source currency. That possibility does not certify the store's runtime implementation. RU/EUR is secondary to the paid pause. Twelve of the store's enabled currencies appear in the opened general table; **BSD, FJD, ISK, KYD, RSD, WST, XCD and XPF** do not. Their Merchant acceptance remains UNRESOLVED for the exact target/source combination; this is no reason to remove Shopify currencies. [Currency conversion](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7055540?hl=en), [landing consistency](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/4752265?hl=en).

**All 21 published languages are named in Merchant's language list.** A language need not be a country's majority language. However, support is not proof that a translated product source exists, that its content is accurate, or that its landing and checkout match. The published `pt-BR` locale uses `/pt/`; its presence does not certify European-Portuguese copy. No new language or country is proposed. [Language requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/160637?hl=en).
