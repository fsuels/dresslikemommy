# US Primary Keyword Lane

Last updated: 2026-05-14

Purpose: make the United States explicit as the first paid-growth keyword/intelligence market. This is local strategy and planning, not live upload approval.

## Position

US is the biggest market and should be the first keyword universe built, scored, and refreshed. GB/CA/AU are important expansion lanes, but they must not cause the operator to skip US demand.

Current live US paid lane in the command layer:

- `Standard Shopping US`
- Shopping has no manual Search keywords.
- Keyword work still matters for US because it informs product titles, feed/title diagnostics, product grouping, Shopping search-term interpretation, Pinterest creative language, and future US Search packets.

## US Language

Use US English first:

- `mom`
- `mommy`
- `mommy and me`
- `mother daughter`
- `mom daughter`
- `family matching`
- `family photos`
- `family pictures`
- `vacation outfits`
- `beach photos`
- `birthday dresses`
- `wedding guest dresses`

Do not replace this with GB/AU `mum/mummy` language for US campaigns.

## US Long-Tail Criteria

Prioritize rows that combine:

- role: mom, mommy, mother, daughter, baby girl, family, dad/daddy where the landing fits
- product: dress, dresses, outfit, pajamas, swimsuit, shirt only when the landing/category matches
- buyer moment: family photos, family pictures, birthday, vacation, beach photo, wedding guest, matching family event
- product detail: color, material, floral, chiffon, ruffle, tulle, summer, beach, holiday

Reject or hold rows that are:

- broad head terms without a buyer moment
- DIY, sewing pattern, free, used, rental, marketplace, local pickup, same-day, adult, doll/game, supplier/source, or inspiration-only
- true for another category but routed to the wrong landing

## Promotion Rule

Build the local US universe as large as possible. Promote live only when:

- first-page estimate is `<= $0.15`, or max CPC `$0.15` has no below-first-page warning
- landing is active, public, purchasable, supplier-clean, country/currency-correct, and category-matched
- query can plausibly convert at about `1.4%` or better toward `650% ROAS`
- owner/campaign surface is clear: Search keyword, Shopping feed/title/product-group, or Pinterest creative/product-group
- reviewer pass and after-state readback plan exist
