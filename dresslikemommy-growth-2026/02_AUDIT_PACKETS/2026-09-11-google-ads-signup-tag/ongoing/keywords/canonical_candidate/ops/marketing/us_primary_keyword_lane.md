# US Primary Keyword Lane

Last updated: 2026-09-11

Purpose: make the United States explicit as the first paid-growth keyword/intelligence market. This is local strategy and planning, not live upload approval.

## Position

US is the primary planning market; current demand and actual economics must determine spending priority. GB/CA/AU are important expansion lanes, but they must not cause the operator to skip US demand.

The former May 14 US Standard Shopping description is historical. Read the present account and campaign scope from the canonical command layer before action; do not recreate an old lane. Shopping and PMax search themes are not manual Search keyword lists. This universe can inform query, product/landing and future Search analysis without a live upload.

## US Long-Tail Criteria

Prioritize rows that combine:

- role: mom, mommy, mother, daughter, baby girl, family, dad/daddy where the landing fits
- product: dress, dresses, outfit, pajamas, swimsuit, shirt only when the landing/category matches
- buyer moment: family photos, family pictures, birthday, vacation, beach photo, wedding guest, matching family event
- product detail: color, material, floral, chiffon, ruffle, tulle, summer, beach, holiday

Reject or hold rows that are:

- broad head terms without a buyer moment
- DIY, sewing-pattern requests, free-product-only intent, used, rental, marketplace, local pickup, same-day, sexually explicit content, doll/game, supplier/source, or inspiration-only
- true for another category but routed to the wrong landing

## Promotion Rule

Build the local US universe as large as possible. Promote live only when:

- current effective CPC controls meet the USD 0.15 ceiling and dated market-specific auction evidence supports a bounded test; an absent warning or historical bid range is not proof
- landing is active, public, purchasable, supplier-clean, country/currency-correct, and category-matched
- return-adjusted actual basket economics meet the stricter of 6.5x ROAS and 30% all-in profit; no fixed AOV or conversion-rate assumption substitutes for evidence
- owner/campaign surface is clear: Search keyword, Shopping feed/title/product-group, or Pinterest creative/product-group
- reviewer pass and after-state readback plan exist

Evidence rule: current command-layer readbacks govern account status and authority. A local score is a research priority, not live readiness. Economic-fit and serveability points require dated evidence for the exact market, keyword and offer; assign zero evidence credit when unknown. Zero credit does not mean zero demand. Recompute the total/threshold without changing keyword identity or promotion status.

Negative intent is not a blanket one-word exclusion: adult sizes are valid apparel; “free shipping” can express buying intent; “pattern” can describe a garment; and “costume/costumi” changes meaning by language. Use original-language, query-specific evidence and check existing negatives before proposing a narrow exclusion.

Economics definitions follow `keyword_strategy.md`: R is retained merchandise plus retained shipping, excluding tax; V is actual variable costs; H is allocated overhead/other costs excluded from V; CM = R − V. Require `A ≤ min(R/6.5, CM − H − 0.30R, approved cash/loss allowance)`. Unknown required inputs remain unqualified; platform value must be reconciled to this basis.
