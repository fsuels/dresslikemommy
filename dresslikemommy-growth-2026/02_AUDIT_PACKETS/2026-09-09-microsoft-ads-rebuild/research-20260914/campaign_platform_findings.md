**PLATFORM FACT — CPC control has an upcoming change.** Microsoft's August31 announcement says October1 removes Max CPC when creating new non-portfolio Maximize Clicks, Maximize Conversions (tCPA) and Maximize Conversion Value (tROAS) campaigns; existing capped campaigns and portfolios retain it. **RECOMMENDATION:** preserve current records; recheck cap retention before any future launch or strategy change. This is not a reason to rush activation. [S01](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/august-2026/ai-max-for-search-and-other-product-news-for-august-2026)

**PLATFORM FACT:** Maximize Clicks' optional limit is documented as a per-click ceiling, unlike eCPC's long-run average objective. Maximize Clicks optimizes clicks, not purchases; keyword bids do not control its auctions. Native strategy/cap readback and eventual billed CPC reconciliation are required. **RECOMMENDATION:** do not substitute eCPC or uncapped value bidding while a hard USD0.15 limit persists. [S02](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/maxclicksbiddingscheme?view=bingads-13), [S03](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_bidstrategy)

**PLATFORM FACT — distribution is broader than the label suggests.** Both distribution options include Search and Audience ads. Microsoft sites and select traffic includes Bing, MSN, Outlook and selected partners, with some domains reported only in aggregate. It is not a pure Bing Search switch. [S04](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutaddistributionhidden) **RECOMMENDATION:** inspect Search versus Audience delivery, publisher, location and query reports separately; use supported exclusions based on real evidence. [S20](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/network?view=bingads-13)

**PLATFORM FACT — international targeting:** Microsoft lists English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Greek and other languages. Ad language differs from interface/billing language; Microsoft does not translate ads automatically. Geographic and language eligibility both apply; a presence-only option exists, with geolocation limitations. PMax language is campaign-level. [S14](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutlanguageoptions), [S15](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_loctargetandlang) **RECOMMENDATION:** qualify each country-language checkout route independently; US Spanish is a separate hypothesis from Spain or Mexico. Platform language/Shopping availability does not prove store delivery, localized product quality or cheap profitable auctions. Do not share an international budget before country contribution differences are known.
