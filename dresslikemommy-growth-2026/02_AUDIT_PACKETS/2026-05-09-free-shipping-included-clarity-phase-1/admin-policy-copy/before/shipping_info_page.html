Shipping Information

At Dress Like Mommy, we are an online store that ships matching family outfits to destinations available at checkout through our shipping and fulfillment partners. Here is how to confirm shipping, delivery timing, and tracking before you place an order.

Free And Paid Shipping Options

Available shipping methods and rates are shown at checkout before payment. Standard shipping is free when a free standard method is shown for your destination. Express or paid options may be available for some addresses.

Where We Ship

Shipping availability is based on the country/region and address entered at checkout. If checkout shows a shipping method for your address, we can ship there under the displayed method and rate.

If your destination does not appear at checkout, or if no shipping method is shown, email us at info@dresslikemommy.com before ordering.

Processing And Delivery Times

Orders are processed within 1-3 business days after payment confirmation. During holidays, promotions, or high-volume periods, processing may take an additional 1-2 business days.

Delivery estimates vary by destination, carrier, customs processing, and the shipping method shown at checkout. Review the shipping method and estimate shown during checkout before placing your order.

Tracking may take 24-48 hours to update after the tracking number is issued.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, check your email for tracking information. You can also visit your account or use the tracking link in the shipping confirmation email when available.

Customs, Duties, And Import Taxes

For orders outside the United States, your destination country or carrier may collect import duties, taxes, brokerage fees, or customs charges. These are the customer's responsibility unless checkout explicitly says they are included.

We cannot predict these charges, mark orders as gifts, or lower the declared value of an order. Contact your local customs office for destination-specific guidance before ordering.

Shipping FAQ

Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
Contact us as soon as possible at info@dresslikemommy.com. If your order has not moved into fulfillment yet, we can review whether an address change is still possible.

Do you ship to P.O. Boxes?
Shipping availability for P.O. Boxes depends on the destination and carrier options shown at checkout.

My package has not arrived yet.
Check your tracking link first. If the tracking has not updated for several business days or the delivery estimate has passed, email us with your order number.
