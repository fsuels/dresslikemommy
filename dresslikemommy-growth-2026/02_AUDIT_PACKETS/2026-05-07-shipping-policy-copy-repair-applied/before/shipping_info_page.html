Shipping Information

At Dress Like Mommy, we ship matching family outfits to families worldwide. Here’s everything you need to know about how your order gets to your door.

📦 Free Shipping on Every Order

We offer FREE standard shipping on all orders — no minimum purchase, no codes needed. It’s our way of making matching outfits accessible to every family.

🌍 Where We Ship

  • United States — all 50 states + territories
  • Canada
  • United Kingdom
  • Australia

Don’t see your country? Email us at info@dresslikemommy.com — we may be able to arrange shipping.

⏰ Processing Time

Orders are processed within 2–3 business days after payment. During busy seasons (holidays, big sales), processing may take an extra day or two.

🚚 Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times after your order ships:

  • United States: 7–12 business days
  • Canada: 10–15 business days
  • United Kingdom: 10–15 business days
  • Australia: 12–20 business days

Note: These are estimates. Actual times may vary due to customs, weather, or carrier delays.

📍 How It Works

We partner with trusted international carriers to ship your outfits. Here’s what to expect:

  1. You order — instant confirmation email
  2. We prepare — your items are carefully packaged (2–3 business days)
  3. We ship — tracking number sent to your email
  4. In transit — track your package anytime
  5. Delivered! — matching outfits at your door

Tip: Tracking may take 24–48 hours to update after you receive the number.

📱 Order Tracking

Every order comes with a tracking number. Once shipped, check your email for:

  • Your tracking number
  • A direct link to track your package
  • Estimated delivery date

🏧 Customs & Duties (International)

For orders outside the US, your country may charge import duties or taxes on delivery. These are not included in our prices and are the buyer’s responsibility. We can’t predict the amount — contact your local customs office for details.

❓ Shipping FAQ

Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
Contact us ASAP at info@dresslikemommy.com. If your order hasn’t shipped yet, we can update it.

Do you ship to P.O. Boxes?
Yes, we ship to P.O. Boxes in the United States.

My package hasn’t arrived yet.
Check your tracking link first. If it’s been significantly longer than the estimated timeframe, email us and we’ll investigate.

Something arrived damaged.
Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We’ll make it right.
