- **Sales and creative source are separate choices.** Pinterest documents Sales with **Pins** for standard ads or **Product catalog** for catalog ads; catalog creative is also supported under Consideration. Sales supports several transactional events, so its name alone does not mean purchase optimization. Select **Checkout** explicitly for completed transactions; **Initiate checkout** measures starting checkout. This distinction is documented, not an inference from the Shopify event name. [Simplified objectives](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/simplified-objectives), [event definitions](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/track-conversions/track-conversions-in-the-api/).

- **Automatic bidding does not require the full Performance+ campaign mode.** Performance+ bidding is a separate Optimization & delivery setting. Performance+ ROAS bidding is explicitly available in traditional campaigns too and requires Checkout events with valid checkout values. Automatic bidding aims for efficient results; it does not guarantee a particular CPC, CPA or ROAS. The simplified-objectives page names Checkout, while the Performance+ setup page uses the broader label Conversion; inspect the actual selected event before certifying purchase optimization. [Bidding](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-your-bid), [Performance+](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-performance-plus).

- **Full Performance+ Sales conflicts with a tightly restricted product/retargeting plan.** Its documented setup uses All Products without a product-group picker, creates prospecting plus Dynamic Retargeting, prevents opting out of that retargeting group during creation, and does not let the advertiser allocate the percentage of spend between groups. These creation limits do not prove every later edit is impossible. Traditional shopping-ad setup documents product-group selection; that is the supported path to investigate for a bounded product cohort. [Performance+](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-performance-plus), [shopping ads](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/shopping-ads).

- **USD5 average daily is not a USD5 daily ceiling.** Performance+ daily budgeting permits higher or lower daily spend and averages over Sunday–Saturday. Pinterest describes 125% as a best effort, so USD6.25 is not a guaranteed daily maximum either. Fixed daily is the documented daily-cap alternative; actual mode and amount must be read back. Full Performance+ campaigns support only Average Daily or Lifetime budgets, so a plan promising both full Performance+ and a fixed USD5 daily cap overstates the documented controls. [Campaign budgets](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-up-campaign-budgets), [Performance+](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-performance-plus).

- **Lifetime and budget edits need separate treatment.** A lifetime budget limits the campaign's total allocation, not equal daily spending. Midweek edits/unpauses restart averaging through Saturday without counting earlier spend; a midday edit can allow spending against both the old and new daily amounts. Also, Pinterest's separate campaign daily/lifetime spend-limit article expressly applies only to video-view objectives. It cannot certify an additional Sales spending cap. Multiple campaigns require an aggregate budget check. [Campaign budgets](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-up-campaign-budgets), [campaign spend limits](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/campaign-details).

Any plan promising reliable cheap purchases merely from selecting Sales or automation needs measured conversion evidence. [Performance+ guidance](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-performance-plus).
