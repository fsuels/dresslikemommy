## F. Paid design and economics

If gates pass, prefer one narrow US Standard Shopping experiment over a catalog-wide PMax launch. Use one ad group and an exact allowlist from one to three qualified product families; exclude everything else from paid targeting. Defer PMax and Demand Gen until a separate, affordable hypothesis warrants them. This preference is a judgment about control and exposure, not proven superiority.

Reconcile historical campaigns and the owner-selected Ads account before creating anything. Do not treat this as the store's first advertising effort or restore old campaigns blindly.

Use one consistent order/cohort basis:

Pre-ad contribution = net merchandise revenue + retained shipping income − disjoint variable non-ad costs.

Profit-preserving ad allowance = pre-ad contribution − required profit − any overhead allocation not already counted.

Marketing-rule allowance = 15% of the agreed revenue basis − other allocated marketing costs.

When both inputs are known, target acquisition allowance = max(0, min(profit-preserving allowance, marketing-rule allowance)). If either allowance is non-positive or unknown, no paid test is qualified. Unknown costs must not be entered as zero expense. Do not double-count refunds already deducted from revenue, provider handling already included in freight, or processing fees already deducted from a payout.

New-customer CAC requires new customers in the denominator and an appropriate expected first-order basket. The store-wide $12.32 figure is a provisional blended per-order allocation.

For planning: affordable CPC ≈ target new-customer CAC × click-to-new-customer conversion rate. At $12.3216, 1% gives $0.123216 and 2% gives $0.246432. Actual bids must also respect the existing $0.15 ceiling and any stricter economic bound. At that ceiling, the illustration requires about 1.22% conversion; this is arithmetic, not a forecast. Check that bid adjustments cannot breach the ceiling.

Use verified shipping geography, presence-oriented location targeting where supported, optional partner expansion initially off, and evidence-based query negatives. Confirm the exact available controls in the selected account. Avoid overlapping Shopping/PMax inventory as an alleged randomized A/B test. No target ROAS guarantees profit.

If using a new campaign total budget, freeze dates, total and monitoring ownership. Any proposal must explicitly resolve applicable project daily caps: total-budget pacing alone cannot enforce them. Total spend is not the same as financial loss; a zero-sale test can lose all media spend plus its operating costs. An average daily budget is not an equivalent finite total cap. Do not relax economic targets merely to spend the allowance.

## G. Measurement acceptance

Trace: Shopify variant → submitted offer → processed Merchant offer/destination → selected page → cart/checkout → purchase → receiving reports → financial reconciliation.

Require accurate item identity, quantity, discounts, value/currency, shipping and returns; correct adult/child and dress/shirt selection; lifecycle updates and withdrawal; supported granted/denied consent behavior; and cancellation/refund reconciliation.

Use one intended purchase conversion path for bidding. Other sources may remain for observation only after inspecting campaign goals: secondary actions included in custom goals can still be used for bidding. Transaction-ID deduplication operates within the same conversion action; it does not fix two primary purchase actions. [Goal behavior](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11461796?hl=en), [deduplication](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6386790?hl=en)

Separate sender/network proof from receiving-report proof and Ads attribution. A normal test purchase without an eligible ad interaction does not establish Ads-attributed reporting. Existing QA traffic is not acquisition.

Document time zones, report dates versus click dates, attribution windows, conversion delays, tax/shipping basis and return lags. The recorded Merchant timezone correction does not reconcile past data automatically.

Google supports conversion restatements/retractions, but the native app's automatic refund-adjustment behavior remains unverified. Confirm the actual supported path before relying on it. [Conversion adjustments](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7686280?hl=en)

Transaction tests require their own approved scope; the owner performs payments. No test order, checkout mutation, consent change or price/stock change was performed in this review.

## H. Optional programs and ongoing management

Promotions require real, affordable offers and consistent pricing. Product Ratings requires at least 50 reviews across products and an eligible participation method; merchant-fed reviews require ongoing compliant updates. Store Quality enrollment follows approved products, while badges require additional performance/eligibility. Neither is a shortcut around receiving zero products. [Ratings basics](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14620705?hl=en), [review-feed policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6098512?hl=en), [Store Quality](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14261098?hl=en)

Defer new paid feed subscriptions, speculative ranking services, new loyalty infrastructure and video production unless a specific limitation or commercial case justifies them. Audit whether default AI direct checkout is actually active before treating it as a future enrollment decision.

During a paid pilot, check spend, receipt failures and critical purchase/offer problems daily. Review queries, product contribution, matching-size gaps and returns weekly; reconcile all marketing and delayed expenses monthly. These cadences do not create a schedule or standing write authority.

Critical tracking, policy, fulfillment or price failures trigger the approved stop procedure. Stop at the finite cap; inconclusive results do not automatically replenish it. If no authorized live action exists, prepare the exact bounded correction and keep valid unpaid work moving.
