# Master All-Markets Approval Phrase

Paste this exact phrase to approve the variant-grouping feed fix across **every active Shopify Market** (US, Canada, United Kingdom, EU, Australia, International) and **every product category** (Family Matching, Dresses, Couples, Sweaters, and any future product types) in a single session:

```
I approve fixing the Shopify -> Pinterest catalog feed for every active Shopify Market (us, canada, united-kingdom, eu, australia, international) and every product category, by enabling parent-product grouping via item_group_id and pinning image_link to the product featured image, with no Shopify product/title/price/inventory/vendor/type/policy edits, no Pinterest campaign/budget/bid/status/audience/tag/CAPI/billing changes, no Merchant Center source/feed mutations beyond what the Shopify channel emits automatically, and no edits to Google & YouTube, Facebook & Instagram, or TikTok sales channels. After the 24-hour Pinterest re-sync, capture before/after readback per market and only then consider promoting/launching. The automated guardrail in ops/scripts/check_pinterest_feed_grouping.py must continue to pass on every future continuity check.
```

## What This Phrase Authorizes

For **every market in scope**:

1. Open `Shopify Admin -> Sales channels -> Pinterest -> Settings`.
2. Change the variant-submission option to the parent-grouping mode (label varies — see per-market packets).
3. Set `image_link` source to product featured image.
4. Keep `additional_image_link` set to variant images.
5. Save.
6. Capture before/after readback per market.

For markets where the Shopify Pinterest channel UI does **not** expose the toggle:

7. Fall back to Path B (custom GraphQL feed generated by `ops/scripts/generate_pinterest_feed_grouped.py` in this packet) for that specific market only.
8. Upload the generated feed file as a separate Pinterest catalog source for that market.

## What This Phrase Does NOT Authorize

- Any Shopify Admin product / title / price / inventory / vendor / type / policy edit.
- Any Pinterest campaign / ad group / ad / budget / bid / status / audience / tag / CAPI / billing change.
- Any Merchant Center source create / delete / re-map.
- Any Google & YouTube, Facebook & Instagram, or TikTok sales-channel setting change.
- Any theme push, file deploy, or destructive filesystem action.
- Any vendor / source URL leak into customer-visible or feed-visible Shopify fields.

## Per-Market Approval Phrases (if you prefer market-by-market)

Each per-market packet under `per_market_packets/` contains a market-specific approval phrase. Use those if you want to fix US first, monitor 24 hours, then approve CA/GB/etc one at a time.

## Stop Conditions (apply to every market)

Stop before any save/publish if:

- Re-authentication / OAuth scope prompt appears.
- Currency / market / region mapping change is requested.
- Billing prompt appears.
- A warning says other sales channels (Google & YouTube, Facebook & Instagram, TikTok) will be affected.
- A modal mentions deleting catalog history.
- CAPTCHA, policy review, or destructive-action confirm appears.
- New severity-1 disapprovals appear after re-sync.

## Rollback

Each per-market packet contains the exact reverse-toggle steps. The fix is reversible at the channel-settings level without touching products or campaigns.
