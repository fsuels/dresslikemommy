Shipping Policy

Last Updated: January 28, 2026

At Dress Like Mommy, we want your matching outfits to arrive as clearly and reliably as possible. Review the shipping method, rate, and delivery estimate shown at checkout before placing an order.

Where We Ship

Shipping is available to the countries and regions shown at checkout. Availability depends on the destination, product, and shipping methods shown during checkout. Use the country/region selector or the checkout shipping step to confirm whether we can ship to your address before placing an order.

If your destination does not appear at checkout, or if no shipping method is shown for your address, contact us at info@dresslikemommy.com before ordering.

Shipping Rates

Standard shipping is included in product prices for countries and regions where a standard method is available. Checkout shows the exact method, delivery estimate, and any express upgrade before payment.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1-3 business days after payment confirmation. During holidays, promotions, or high-volume periods, processing may take an additional 1-2 business days.

You will receive an email with tracking information once your order ships.

Delivery Times

Orders are processed within 1-3 business days after payment confirmation. Delivery estimates vary by destination, carrier, customs processing, and the shipping method shown at checkout.

  • Standard Delivery: the current checkout estimate displays before payment.
  • Express Delivery: available for some destinations where shown at checkout.

These are estimates. Actual delivery times may vary because of customs processing, carrier delays, weather, holidays, or local conditions.

How Our Shipping Works

  1. Order Placed: you receive an order confirmation email.
  2. Order Processing: your order is prepared after payment confirmation.
  3. Shipped: tracking information is emailed when available.
  4. In Transit: use the tracking link for carrier updates.
  5. Delivered: your package is delivered to the address provided at checkout.

Order Tracking

Every order includes a tracking number when the carrier provides one. Tracking may take 24-48 hours to update after the number is issued.

You can also check your order status anytime by visiting our Order Status page.

Customs, Duties, And Import Taxes

For orders shipped outside the United States, your destination country or carrier may collect import duties, taxes, brokerage fees, or customs charges. These charges are the customer's responsibility unless checkout explicitly says they are included.

We cannot predict these charges, mark orders as gifts, or lower the declared value of an order. Contact your local customs office for destination-specific guidance before ordering.

Shipping Problems

If your package is delayed, missing, or marked as delivered but not received, contact us at info@dresslikemommy.com with your order number and tracking information. We will help review the carrier information and next available steps.

Contact Us

For shipping questions, email info@dresslikemommy.com.

    Family Matching Pajamas: Our Top Picks for Cozy Nights

    December 8, 2024 Jessica Martinez Family Fashion Editor
    4 min read
    Share

    In this article

      There’s nothing quite like the whole family matching in cozy pajamas. Whether it’s movie night, Christmas morning, or a lazy Sunday, family matching pajamas turn an ordinary evening into something special. And honestly? They make for the best photos you’ll ever take.

      From holiday traditions to everyday comfort, this guide covers the best matching pajama styles, fabrics, and tips to help your family find the perfect sets.

      Why Matching Pajamas Are a Family Must-Have

      Matching pajamas have gone from a holiday novelty to a year-round family tradition. Here’s why everyone’s doing it:

      • Instant bonding — there’s something about wearing the same PJs that makes everyone feel closer
      • Photo-ready — matching jammies are the easiest way to get a great family photo without anyone complaining about what to wear
      • Holiday traditions — Christmas Eve in matching PJs has become THE family tradition
      • Gift-worthy — matching pajama sets make fantastic gifts for holidays, baby showers, and family reunions
      • Kids love it — seriously, try telling your kid they get to match with mom AND dad. Watch their face light up.

      Best Styles of Family Matching Pajamas

      Classic Long-Sleeve Sets

      The gold standard. Long-sleeve top with matching pants in coordinating prints. These work year-round and come in everything from plaid to florals to fun characters. Perfect for cooler nights and the go-to choice for holiday photos. Browse our full pajama collection for classic sets the whole family will love.

      Short-Sleeve Pajama Sets

      For warmer climates or summer nights. Short-sleeve matching sets are lighter and more comfortable when the temperature rises. Tropical prints, bright colors, and playful patterns shine in summer PJ sets. Great for family vacations and sleepovers.

      Onesies and Footie Pajamas

      Yes, they make adult onesies — and they’re amazing. Matching family onesies for the holidays have become hugely popular on social media. Baby in a footie, toddler in a onesie, and mom and dad in matching adult versions? Peak family content.

      Mommy-and-Daughter Matching Pajamas

      For a mother-daughter bedtime routine or a coordinated photo, browse our mommy-and-me pajamas. Check the available adult and child sizes on each product page, then choose the right option for each person. Read the product details to confirm what each selection includes.

      Best Fabrics for Family Pajamas

      Not all pajamas are created equal. Here’s what to look for:

      • Cotton — breathable, soft, and great for year-round wear. The classic choice.
      • Bamboo — incredibly soft, temperature-regulating, and eco-friendly. Premium feel.
      • Flannel — warm and cozy for winter nights. The traditional holiday pajama fabric.
      • Jersey knit — stretchy and comfortable. Like wearing your favorite t-shirt to bed.
      • Fleece — ultra-warm for cold winter nights. Best for onesies and cold climates.

      For kids: Choose size-appropriate sleepwear and follow its safety label and intended fit. Check the product details rather than assuming that a fabric or a daytime outfit is suitable for sleeping. The CPSC children’s sleepwear guide explains why oversized, loose-fitting cotton sleepwear can be a hazard.

      Matching Pajamas for Every Occasion

      Christmas Morning

      Coordinating pajamas can be part of a Christmas-morning photo or breakfast tradition without a holiday-specific print. Browse our currently available matching pajamas and check the sizes offered for each person before choosing a design.

      Halloween

      Spooky-themed matching pajamas — think skeletons, bats, pumpkins, and glow-in-the-dark prints. Perfect for Halloween movie nights and trick-or-treat prep.

      Valentine’s Day

      Heart prints and pink-red color schemes for the cutest family Valentine’s photos. See our matching outfits for Valentine’s options beyond pajamas.

      Everyday / No Occasion Needed

      You don’t need a holiday to match. Solid colors, subtle stripes, or fun all-over prints work for any night of the week. Make “Family PJ Night” a weekly tradition — your kids will remember it forever.

      How to Get the Whole Family On Board

      Getting everyone to match can be tricky (especially teenagers). Here are some tips:

      1. Let everyone pick the pattern — take a family vote on which print to get
      2. Start with holidays — “Just for Christmas morning” is an easy sell
      3. Make it fun — pair matching PJs with movie night, hot cocoa, or breakfast in bed
      4. Include the pets — yes, matching pet pajamas exist and they’re amazing
      5. Make it a tradition — once you start, everyone will look forward to it

      Sizing Tips for Family Pajama Sets

      The trickiest part of matching pajamas is getting the sizing right for everyone:

      • Kids: Compare current measurements with the product’s size chart and follow the intended fit and safety label. Do not automatically size up
      • Adults: Check the size chart — PJ sizing varies more than regular clothes
      • Babies: Choose sleepwear that fits now, using the size chart and label guidance; do not buy oversized sleepwear to grow into
      • Before ordering: Check that every person’s size is available and select each wearer’s option separately

      Care Tips to Keep Matching PJs Looking Fresh

      • Wash inside out in cold water to preserve prints
      • Avoid bleach — it fades matching colors unevenly
      • Tumble dry low or hang dry for longest life
      • Buy two sets if you want them to last all season (rotation = less washing)
