Plan each person's outfit before you order

Make one line for each person in your photo: the piece they will wear, their selected size, the quantity you need, and anything they already own that coordinates. Start with one print or color you love, then build around it.

  1. Choose the pieces. Decide who needs a dress, a shirt, or another item. Check what each product selection includes before adding it to your bag.
  2. Check every person's size. Open the size chart on that exact product page. Compare the measurements and units shown; use age labels as a starting point. Different styles can use different size ranges.
  3. Count the items. For dresses sold separately, add a mother size and a daughter size as separate selections. Review each line and the combined price in your bag.
  4. Check timing for your destination. Read the current delivery estimate and shipping information before ordering, and allow time to try everything on. If your photo date is close, use an outfit you already have.
  5. Finish the look. Lay the outfits together with shoes and any layers you own. Check that everyone can sit, walk, and move comfortably.
