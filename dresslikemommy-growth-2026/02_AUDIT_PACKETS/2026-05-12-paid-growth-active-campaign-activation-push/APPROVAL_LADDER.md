# Approval Ladder Toward Active Campaigns

This file separates the owner goal from the live-write gates. It is not a request to slow down; it is the switchboard for moving fast without accidental spend, duplicate tracking, bad product scope, or unmeasured non-US traffic.

## 1. Measurement Gate

Use when read-only GA4 proof cannot close the non-US currency/value question and the owner wants to unblock activation by generating a real controlled signal.

```text
APPROVE CONTROLLED NON-US MEASUREMENT TEST PURCHASE ONLY: PERFORM ONE LOW-RISK NON-US CHECKOUT TEST USING A COUNTRY/CURRENCY NAMED IN THE PRECHECK, CAP TOTAL PRODUCT+SHIPPING+TAX/DUTY EXPOSURE AT THE LOWEST PRACTICAL AMOUNT, DO NOT ENABLE OR EDIT ANY ADS, MERCHANT, PINTEREST, PRODUCT SCOPE, FEED LABEL, PRODUCT GROUP, CONVERSION GOAL, BUDGET, BID, OR CAMPAIGN STATUS; CAPTURE GA4/GOOGLE ADS PURCHASE TRANSACTION/CURRENCY/VALUE READBACKS; THEN REFUND/CANCEL/VOID ACCORDING TO THE SAFEST AVAILABLE SHOPIFY PAYMENT PATH; DOCUMENT ALL STEPS AND STOP IF ANY PAYMENT/ORDER RISK EXCEEDS THE PRECHECK.
```

## 6. What Is Not Approved By A Goal Statement Alone

The statement "make all campaigns active" does not by itself authorize:
- Enabling paused campaigns.
- Creating live spend.
- Changing budgets, bids, statuses, product scope, feed labels, product groups, or conversion goals.
- Uploading Merchant feeds or editing Merchant sources.
- Creating Pinterest account objects, product groups, campaigns, or live spend.
- Creating orders/payments/refunds/cancellations.
- Editing Shopify product data, markets, shipping, discounts, or checkout.

Those actions need exact action-time approval because they are externally visible, spend-bearing, or production-mutating.
