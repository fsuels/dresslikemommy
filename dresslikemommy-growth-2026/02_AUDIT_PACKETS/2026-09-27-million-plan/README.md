## 1. The business

- **Model:** a Shopify store (dresslikemommy.com, Dawn-derived theme released from GitHub `main`) selling matching family outfits by relationship and occasion: Mommy & Me, family matching, Daddy & Me, couples, pajamas, swim, dresses, Christmas and Halloween.
- **Dropshipping, no inventory:** supplier products come mainly from 1688, with BuckyDrop as the purchasing agent. BuckyDrop can also buy from Taobao, Tmall, JD and others.
  - Orders are consolidated at BuckyDrop's Huizhou (Guangdong) warehouse, then shipped by YunExpress: Registered Air Mail for the US, Air Cargo for EU/UK/AU/CA.
  - Delivery is about 12–16 days. Shipping is free for the customer; freight is built into the price.
- **Markets:** 20+ languages and localized prices. Markets price lists add +15% for EU and UK and +20% for AU on top of base prices (applied 2026-09-27).
- **Pricing rule (owner):**
  - Landed cost ≤ 50% of price, and net ≥ 35% after marketing, refunds and fees.
  - BuckyDrop shortcut: price = (cost CNY + domestic) / 7.11 × 4.2, compare-at = price + $10.
  - Claude's floor: price ≥ max(4.2 × cost, 2 × cost + $11.50) for a two-piece order.
  - US freight ≈ ¥45.4 + ¥8.2 per 100 g.
- **Listing workflow:**
  - Canonical DRAFT → 100 stock per size → 4 photoshoot images (IMAGE 1, 3, 5 and 6) generated by the ChatGPT app's Codex on the owner's Pro plan, never the API → review against the vendor photos → Claude activates via `activate_listing.py`.
  - Only current-year designs (the release-year attribute) from gate-passing suppliers (`ops/sourcing/`).
