# Dress Like Mommy: free Christmas 2026 exposure targets

Research date: 2026-09-27. This was read-only research: I contacted no one, signed up for nothing and submitted no forms.

**Confidence key**
- **H**: I loaded the page this session and the details came from it.
- **M**: The page loaded but some details were missing, or they came from a search-result snippet or a third-party review.
- **L**: The page did not load (403, 429 or 503). The entry rests on search results only.

**Before any outreach, the owner needs to know four things:**
1. **Pitching is customer-facing messaging.** Under the repo rules it needs the owner's explicit yes.
2. **Most free gift guides want a product sample, and that costs money.** The store is dropship, so each sample is paid product plus shipping and takes 12–16 days to arrive. That needs a money approval, and samples have to be ordered weeks before each deadline.
3. **Every pitch should state an order-by date.** With 12–16 day delivery, Christmas orders need to be in around the first week of December. Never claim fast shipping, local stock or a warehouse.
4. **Many roundups list only big brands (marked "Big-only" below).** They are still valid targets for their 2026 update, but the chance of getting in is lower. Entries marked **Small ✓** already feature independent or Etsy shops.

## Summary: the 10 to pitch first

1. **Pitch the open 2026 calls first: Emily Reviews and The Review Wire.** Both are open now, free to submit and aimed at US parents, with deadlines (Nov 15 ideal, Dec 7 final) the 12–16 day shipping can still meet if the samples are approved this week.
2. **Then the three small-shop-friendly writers: Sweaty as a Mother, Friday We're In Love and Hello! Happiness.** Sweaty as a Mother explicitly asks small businesses to email in. The other two published 2025 pajama or holiday guides that already include small or Etsy brands and have public brand contacts.
3. **Then Mom Does Reviews (#MegaChristmas26 is live, plus an Aug 2026 Christmas pajamas guide) and Jenn Hallak (an annual matching-pajamas post with a business email).** Both write about exactly this product every year.
4. **Subscribe to HARO and Source of Sources the same day.** Both are free and cost nothing but time. Answer only genuine gift-guide or family-pajama queries (SOS removes you for sales pitches).
5. **Round out with Caitlin Houston** (Christmas pajama photo content, updated Dec 2025), a strong fit for the "family photo outfit" angle.

All outreach needs the owner's explicit yes (customer messaging), and any samples need a money approval. Order samples early because of the 12–16 day delivery.
