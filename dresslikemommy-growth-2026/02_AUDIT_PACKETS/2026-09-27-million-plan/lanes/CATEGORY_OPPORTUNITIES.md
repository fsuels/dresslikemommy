## Avoid

- **Loose kids' pajamas and nightgowns.** Sizes 0–14 must be tight-fitting (with the snug-fit hangtag) or flame-resistant ([CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Childrens-Sleepwear)). This covers the girl nightgowns in merch.md Brief 2. `OWNER_DECISION`.
- **CPSIA children's product certificates** are a gap across the whole catalog.
- **IP:** licensed characters, school or team logos (drop the school-spirit tees), and look-alike brand prints.
- **Unsupported claims:** calling a dress "maternity" when it isn't a maternity cut, "vet-approved", or holiday arrival promised after the last-order date.
- **Low-value categories:** Thanksgiving, Halloween costumes, tees under $24.99, twins-only lines, and pet items sold without a matching human line.
