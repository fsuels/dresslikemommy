# Fast-Ship Christmas Vendor Memo (2026-09-27)

**Bottom line:** No US all-over-print (AOP) pajama option meets both landed cost ≤50% and delivery in 7 days or less. The fast lane that works is **matching DTG Christmas sweatshirts plus a baby bodysuit, from Printify Premium on one US provider**. It moves the Christmas order-by date from Dec 8 to **~Dec 10 (standard) and ~Dec 15 (paid priority)**.

**Family basket** (2 adults + 1 youth + 1 toddler, Premium): product $50.49 + shipping ~$18 + $0.40 = **$68.90 landed on $149.96 revenue (46%)**. After ~3% payment fees and ads at 650% ROAS (15.4%), **net ≈35–36%**. A single adult order nets ≈33% (borderline). The toddler piece is the costliest ($15.30).

## Ranked recommendation
1. **Printify Premium + one US provider for DTG family sweatshirts.** SwiftPOD is the candidate: its 2025 cutoffs were Dec 10 standard and Dec 15 priority, with 2–5 business days of production. Check that one provider carries adult 18000, youth 18000B, toddler Rabbit Skins 3317 and baby 4411 in the same colors. Otherwise each family order ships as several parcels, each paying a first-item fee.
2. **CJ US-warehouse private stock of the 2 top 1688 pajama designs.** Only if the owner accepts inventory risk. It breaks pure dropship, needs a 30% deposit and sea freight before late October, and anything unsold after Dec 25 is dead stock. It would allow an order-by date of ~Dec 17. Get a quote first.

**Not recommended:** AOP POD pajamas; Printful (margin); Merchize (speed); Faire (wholesale, not dropship); Spocket and AliExpress Choice US (no verified US stock of family pajamas).

## Pilot
- **3 original designs:** "Mama/Papa/Mini/Baby Claus"; a Fair Isle reindeer; a plaid-block "Family Christmas 2026".
- **Sizes:** adult S–3XL, youth XS–XL, toddler 2T–5/6T, baby long-sleeve bodysuit NB–18M. **Colors:** red, forest green, heather grey.
- **Prices:** adult $39.99; youth and toddler $34.99; baby $29.99 (~$15 landed).
- **Shipping:** free standard; paid Priority at ~$12.99 (Printify's cost ≈ $9.99–14.99).
- **Delivery:** standard 4–10 business days (7 days not guaranteed); priority ~4–8. **Order-by:** Dec 10 / Dec 15. Re-check when Printify publishes 2026 cutoffs (usually mid-October).
- **Gate:** live by Oct 15. Kill if landed cost is above 50% or ROAS is below 4 by Nov 25.
- **Copy:** "made to order". Say "printed in USA" only if the facility is in the US (Monster Digital also runs a plant in Juarez, Mexico). No warehouse or stock claims.

## Risks
- **Kids sleepwear law (16 CFR 1615/1616):** sleepwear in sizes 9M–14 must be flame-resistant or tight-fitting. Loose POD "kids pajamas" are a compliance risk, and this also applies to the current catalog. Sweatshirts are not sleepwear.
- **AOP quality:** thin polyester, white creases at seams, sizes run small.
- **IP:** Printify blocks infringing designs. No Grinch, Elf or other licensed characters.
- **Peak season:** Printify says to use its cutoffs "with caution". A $0.40 surcharge applies per US order.
- **Prices:** the Premium prices come from catalog listings; check them in the app.

## Owner must do
1. Create the Printify account (owner login).
2. Approve and install the Printify Shopify app (OAuth).
3. Add a billing card and approve **Premium at $299/yr** (or $39/mo). It breaks even at ~75 sweatshirts, saving ~$4 each.
4. Approve a **sample order of ~$70–90** (one adult, youth, toddler and baby piece).
5. Approve a Shopify shipping profile for POD items: free standard plus paid priority.
6. Optional, for option 2: a CJ account and quote. Any deposit is a money move.
