Verified: 2026-05-06, America/New_York

## Executive Decision

Move faster, but do not confuse more campaigns with more profit.

The expert path today is to launch or prepare the highest-intent, lowest-waste layers first:

1. Repair Brand Search drift so brand demand is protected cheaply.
2. Enable warm remarketing only if policy/audience/readback is clean, because it is the cheapest near-buyer traffic.
3. Build a new paused US nonbrand Search campaign with tight exact/phrase ad groups and strong RSAs, replacing the paused poor generic build.
4. Keep Standard Shopping running under the new lower base CPC bids and monitor fresh CPC/purchase value.
5. Run Pinterest account/tag/catalog readback before any spend, then build paused product-group tests if the gate passes.
6. Prepare CA/UK/AU expansion as paused/readiness work, not live spend, until shipping, checkout, currency, returns, and landing pages are confirmed.

Do not launch Denmark, Switzerland, or broad multilingual paid campaigns today. Recent orders from those countries are useful demand signals, but the live shipping/localization proof is not yet strong enough to buy cold paid traffic there professionally.

## Why This Is The Fastest Safe Growth Path

The owner target economics are strict:

- Example AOV: about `$70`
- Gross margin before ads/returns: about `50%`
- Target ROAS: `650%`
- Target CPA: `$70 / 6.5 = $10.77`

At that CPA, a `$0.20` click needs roughly a `1.9%` purchase conversion rate before return friction. That means cheap traffic alone is not the goal; cheap qualified traffic is the goal.

The priority order is therefore:

1. Hot traffic: Brand Search and remarketing.
2. High-intent cold traffic: tight US nonbrand Search and controlled Shopping.
3. Visual discovery: Pinterest, capped and measured.
4. International expansion: English shipping-supported markets first, then localized markets after QA.
